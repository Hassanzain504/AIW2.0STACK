8. "made a stressful day much easier"

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Binding or flat-rate quote statement** (moving has one of the worst reputations for surprise charges -- "quote doubles on moving day" is the #1 horror story; a plain "we give you a binding price before moving day" statement is the highest-converting trust signal in this niche)
2. **Named crew with background checks** (homeowners are handing strangers access to every item they own, often while their family is present; a crew profile with background check badge converts the homeowner who is comparing multiple movers)
3. **No-damage guarantee or insurance coverage statement** (damage to furniture and walls is the second most common complaint; a plain statement about coverage and what happens if something breaks removes the hesitation of the homeowner with expensive furniture)
