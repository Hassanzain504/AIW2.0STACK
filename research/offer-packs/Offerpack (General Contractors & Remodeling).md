Hassan has 4 years of freelance web dev experience but no GC-specific case studies and no existing client in this niche. The correct offer shape is:

**Risk reversal:** Free homepage design upfront. Paid on approval only. First client pays nothing until they see the design and say yes.

**Price anchor (3-tier, they buy the middle):**

| Tier | What they get | Price |

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Project gallery with cost ranges and project descriptions** (remodeling buyers are imagining their own home -- a gallery labeled by project type and investment range converts the prospect who is researching whether they can afford the project)
2. **Written contract with payment schedule before any work starts** (GC horror stories dominate negative reviews -- deposit taken, project abandoned, cost overruns -- one sentence addressing this removes the #1 barrier to calling)
3. **Named project manager with contact info** (homeowners living through a remodel want a direct line to the person running the job, not a general inbox; putting the PM's name and number on the site is a differentiator no competitor offers)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
