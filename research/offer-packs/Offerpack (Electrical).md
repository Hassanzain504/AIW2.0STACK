## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **License number and insurance visible in the hero** (electrical work involves safety and permits; showing the master electrician license number is the single highest-converting trust signal in this niche -- homeowners fear unlicensed work failing inspection or causing fires)
2. **Upfront written quote before any work starts** (surprise charges on electrical jobs are the most common complaint -- one sentence removing that fear converts better than any other copy on the page)
3. **Permit-pulling statement** (many homeowners don't know their contractor is supposed to pull permits; an electrician who explicitly says "we pull all required permits" differentiates from the unlicensed competition instantly)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
