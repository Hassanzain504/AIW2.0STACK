## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Before/after repair gallery showing texture match** (drywall is an invisibility skill -- homeowners want to see a repaired wall that looks like it was never touched; the gallery that shows the same wall before and after the repair is the highest-converting image in this niche)
2. **Texture matching capability statement** (the #1 complaint in negative drywall reviews is visible texture mismatch; a plain "we match your existing texture -- skip trowel, knockdown, smooth, orange peel" statement removes the #1 hesitation)
3. **Dust control and cleanup statement** (drywall dust is a homeowner's nightmare; the contractor who explicitly addresses cleanup before the homeowner asks converts better than the one who doesn't mention it)
