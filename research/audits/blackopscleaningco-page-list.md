## Two rules that override this list

1. **No town page without a real job in that town.** If Black Ops has only worked in five
   towns, build five town pages, not seventeen. Six real pages beat seventeen invented ones.
2. **A service plus town page only after the plain service page is live and ranking.** Never
   build `/biohazard-cleanup-bethesda-md/` before `/services/biohazard-remediation/` exists and
   has settled. That is why all 19 are Phase 3.
