## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Before/after curb appeal gallery** (siding is the most visual exterior purchase a homeowner makes -- the gallery that shows the same house before and after, in the same light, converts the undecided homeowner faster than any other signal)
2. **Material comparison section** (fiber cement vs vinyl vs engineered wood -- homeowners comparing siding options are doing research; the site that helps them choose gets the call, not the site that just says "we install all types")
3. **Insurance claim assistance statement** (hail and wind damage are the biggest siding lead sources -- the contractor who mentions "we help with the insurance claim process" on the homepage wins the post-storm call)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.
