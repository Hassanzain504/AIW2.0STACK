## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Plain-language service explainer pages** (septic and well work is technical and unfamiliar to most homeowners, so sites that explain what a pump-out or well inspection actually involves convert far better than those that just list services)
2. **Emergency availability statement with a real phone number** (a failed septic system or dry well is an emergency, and homeowners searching at 9pm need to see "24/7 emergency service" above the fold)
3. **Licensed and certified badge with state license number** (this niche involves health regulations and permits, so license credentials displayed visibly are the single strongest trust signal)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
