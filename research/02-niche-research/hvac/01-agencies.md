## Agency Profiles

### 1. Scorpion.co (HVAC vertical)
**URL:** scorpion.co/hvac/
**Positioning:** "Full schedules. No matter the season. Grow your HVAC business with marketing that makes you the #1 choice."
**Approach:** Full-platform digital marketing ("RevenueMAX") combining website, SEO, PPC, reputation management, AI call answering, and appointment scheduling. Heavy technology layer: AI voice assistant qualifies and books calls, messaging assistant handles after-hours leads.
**Pricing:** Not publicly listed. [inferred] $2,000-$6,000/month for full platform.

**Pros:**
- Platform depth: lead capture, qualification, booking, and follow-up in one system
- Seasonal framing ("Full schedules. No matter the season.") directly addresses HVAC's primary business risk
- AI tools address after-hours lead loss — a real HVAC pain point (breakdowns happen at night)
- Converts leads with "quick responses and easy online scheduling"

**Cons:**
- Platform lock-in; contractors depend entirely on Scorpion's infrastructure
- High price point; not accessible to early-stage HVAC businesses
- Technology-led pitch may not resonate with owner-operator contractors who are skeptical of "AI"
- Website design produced for clients is functional but template-heavy

**What they miss:** Scorpion's copy targets the HVAC contractor ("grow your business," "full schedules"). Their client sites do not show visible investment in homeowner-facing copy: explaining systems, framing seasonal urgency, or addressing the homeowner's fear of a $15,000 system replacement. The technology layer is impressive but the homeowner experience is generic.

## Top 3 Winner Patterns

1. **Platform depth wins at scale.** Scorpion wins because they solve the full lead cycle: capture, qualify, book, follow up, and track to revenue. HVAC contractors who have been burned by leads that go unanswered value an all-in-one system.

2. **Seasonal language resonates.** HVAC contractors live in fear of slow shoulder seasons. Agencies that frame their pitch around "full schedules year-round" or "no matter the season" address the primary business anxiety directly.

3. **Technology as a differentiator.** AI call answering and instant messaging response (Scorpion's features) address a real HVAC-specific problem: breakdowns happen after hours and homeowners call whoever answers first. Agencies that automate first response win contractor trust.

---

## Top 3 Loser Patterns

1. **Empathy misdirected at the contractor.** Agency copy describes the contractor's pain (seasonality, labor costs, regulation). None translate this into a homeowner-facing message. The contractor's seasonal problem is the homeowner's opportunity ("serviced before the heat hits — schedule now").

2. **No system-education content on client sites.** HVAC homeowners are making $8,000-$15,000 decisions without understanding their system. Contractors whose sites explain system lifespans, efficiency ratings, and replacement decision triggers build trust before a competitor even gets to the quote stage.

3. **Generic trust signals.** "Licensed and insured" appears on every HVAC site. NATE certification (the HVAC-specific credential most homeowners can look up) is often buried or absent from the hero. The credentials that HVAC homeowners actually value are under-utilized.
