1. Scope of work

1.1

The website

The Provider will correct every technical fault identified in the audit of blackopscleaningco.com, including compressing and converting images, bringing Core Web Vitals to green on mobile, adding a main heading to every page, rewriting page titles and descriptions, removing the CDC, EPA and OSHA agency logos, adding structured business data, rebuilding the enquiry form to qualify commercial leads, and installing call tracking and conversion tracking.

1.2

The pages

The Provider will write and build 57 pages: 13 service pages, 17 town pages, 19 pages combining a service with a town, and 8 supporting pages. Fourteen priority pages will be live within the thirty day schedule. The remaining 43 will be released on a rolling schedule of two to three per week until all 57 are published, at no additional cost.

The Client acknowledges that town pages require a genuine completed job in that town, a photograph from it, and a local detail, and that pages for towns where the Client has not worked will be deferred until such a job exists.

1.3

Google Business Profile

The Provider will create or rebuild and fully optimise the Client's Google Business Profile, including category research, service area configuration, all services written as separate items with descriptions, the Products section, business description, attributes, hours, twenty photographs, and seeded questions and answers. The Provider will also merge the duplicate Facebook page and repair the Chamber of Commerce listing.

1.4

Review system

The Provider will build a review generation system: a competitor benchmark establishing the Client's real review target, a direct review link and QR code, a written technician script, an SMS sequence with one reminder, response templates for positive and negative reviews, and the handling of all review replies for the first month.

1.5

Citations

The Provider will create between 30 and 40 business listings with identical details throughout, audit existing listings, remove duplicates, and claim unclaimed profiles.

2. Fees and payment

2.1

Fee

The total fee is three thousand three hundred United States dollars ($3,300 USD), fixed. The fee does not change if the work requires more hours than estimated.

2.2

Schedule

Fifty percent ($1,650) is payable on signing this agreement, to begin work. The remaining fifty percent ($1,650) is payable on completion of the thirty day schedule.

2.3

Method and charges

Payment is by bank transfer to the account detailed on invoice BOC-001. All bank charges, intermediary fees and currency conversion costs are payable by the Client in addition to the fee. The Client should quote the reference BOC-001 on each transfer.

2.4

Late payment

Where the second payment remains outstanding more than fourteen days after it falls due, the Provider may pause the rolling release of remaining pages until payment is received. Work already delivered is not withdrawn.

3. Timeline

3.1

Duration

Thirty days, in three stages: days 1 to 10, days 11 to 19, and days 20 to 30. The rolling release of the remaining pages continues after day 30 until all 57 are published.

3.2

Start date

The thirty day period begins on the later of: the date the first payment is received, or the date full account access is handed over.

3.3

Delays

Delays caused by late account access, late approval of drafts, missing photographs, or any other client-supplied material extend the schedule by the length of the delay. Delays caused by third party platform verification, including Google Business Profile verification, extend the schedule and are not a breach by either party.

4. Client obligations

4.1

Access and information

The Client will provide, within the first week: access to the Google Business Profile, Google Search Console, Google Analytics, website administration, hosting, and domain or DNS; licence and insurance numbers; SAM.gov details; a business email inbox the Provider can reach; and a telephone number that will be answered for automated verification calls.

4.2

Photographs

The Client will supply a minimum of five photographs of completed work each week. The Client acknowledges that these are required to replace stock photography and to enable town pages, and that the rate at which remaining pages can be published depends on them.

4.3

Reviews

The Client acknowledges that business to business reviews cannot be generated by the Provider and must come from the Client personally contacting past and current customers. The Client agrees to make these approaches. The Client acknowledges that Google Maps visibility depends materially on review volume and frequency, and that failure to generate reviews will limit results regardless of the Provider's work.

4.4

Decisions

The Client will confirm, within the first week: whether the business will answer telephone calls twenty-four hours a day, and whether the business positions itself as commercial and specialty or also residential. Emergency response pages will not be built where the Client does not commit to twenty-four hour telephone cover.

4.5

Approvals

The Client will respond to drafts within three working days.

5. Guarantees and disclaimers

5.1

Delivery guarantee

Any figures discussed in proposals, audits or meetings are published industry benchmarks or illustrative estimates and do not constitute a forecast, representation or warranty of results.

5.3

Third party platforms

The work depends on platforms operated by third parties including Google, Meta, Yelp and various business directories. Account suspensions, policy changes, verification delays, rejected listings and service outages on those platforms are outside the control of either party and do not constitute a breach of this agreement.
