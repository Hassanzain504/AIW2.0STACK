Questions

What homeowners ask us

How often should my septic tank be pumped?

For most homes, every 3 to 5 years. A bigger family, a smaller tank, or a garbage disposal means more often. After we pump, we tell you when to book the next one.

How much does septic pumping cost?

It depends on tank size, how easy the lid is to reach, and how long since the last pumping. Call us with your town and tank size if you know it, and we give you the price on the phone before we come out.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. If the lid is uncovered or marked and we can reach the tank, you do not need to be there. We will call you with what we found.

I do not know where my tank lid is. Is that a problem?

No. We find it. Tell us when you book so we can plan the time.

My drains are backing up. What should I do right now?

Stop running water, including laundry and the dishwasher. Then call us at 847-426-5769.

Will pumping fix a soggy drain field?

Sometimes it helps, sometimes the problem is in the lines or the field itself. We check and tell you honestly which it is before you spend money.

I am selling my house. Do I need a septic inspection?

Many buyers and lenders ask for one. Book early so the paperwork is ready before closing.

Are additives worth buying?

For most homes, no. Regular pumping does more for your system than anything poured down the drain.
