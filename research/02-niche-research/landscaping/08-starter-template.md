# 08 - Starter Design Template
Niche: landscaping
Module: 2B, Sub-task 8 (chosen niche only)

---

## Page structure

This is the wireframe blueprint for every landscaping contractor site Hassan builds. It handles both buyer journeys (maintenance and design/install) via a single homepage with a split CTA and two conversion flows.

---

## Homepage section order

1. **Hero**
   - Background: real job photo (before/after pair or dramatic finished yard)
   - H1: "Your Vision. Our Work. [City]'s Landscaping Specialists."
   - Subhead: "From lawn care plans to full backyard transformations — we document every project."
   - Primary CTA: "See Our Work" (anchors to gallery)
   - Secondary CTA: "Get a Free Estimate" (opens inline form)
   - Trust bar below hero: [Years in business] | [Number of projects completed] | [City] based | Licensed and insured

2. **Social proof strip**
   - 3 Google review pull-quotes, each with reviewer first name and star rating
   - "4.9 stars across [N] Google reviews" with a link to Google listing
   - One short video testimonial embed if available

3. **What we do (split buyer journey)**
   - Two cards side by side:
     - Card 1: "Weekly Lawn Care" — price anchor ("Starting at $X/visit"), bulleted services, CTA: "Get a Maintenance Quote"
     - Card 2: "Landscape Design + Install" — project type list (patios, planting beds, lighting, full transformations), CTA: "Start a Project"
   - This is the single most important structural decision in the template. It separates the two buyer journeys before they get confused.

4. **Project gallery (filterable)**
   - Filter tabs: All / Lawn Care / Patios + Hardscape / Planting + Beds / Full Transformations / Before + After
   - Each photo card: project type label, approximate investment range ("$8,000-$14,000"), neighborhood or city area, short descriptor
   - This section is the primary conversion driver. Quality and specificity here matters more than any other section.

5. **Deposit protection statement**
   - Standalone section, not buried in footer
   - H2: "Your Deposit Is Protected"
   - Body: "Every project starts with a written contract that specifies scope, timeline, payment schedule, and what happens if anything changes. You know exactly what you're paying for before any work begins."
   - This section directly addresses the #1 homeowner fear in this niche. No competitor includes it. It will be read by every homeowner who has heard a horror story.

6. **Meet the team**
   - Owner headshot + short bio (2-3 sentences: how long in business, what they care about, local connection)
   - 2-3 crew member profiles with first name and role
   - Real photos only — no stock

7. **How it works**
   - 3 steps for design/install: (1) Free yard assessment, (2) Design proposal with visuals, (3) Build on your timeline
   - 3 steps for maintenance: (1) Free quote, (2) Schedule your first visit, (3) Consistent service every week
   - Keep it scannable — icon + one line per step

8. **Service area map or city list**
   - Local SEO signal + homeowner reassurance that you serve their area
   - Include 5-10 specific neighborhoods or suburbs if targeting a metro

9. **FAQ**
   - 6-8 questions targeting the homeowner's decision moment:
     - "How much does a backyard transformation cost?"
     - "Do you offer financing?"
     - "What if I need to change the design after we start?"
     - "How long does a typical installation take?"
     - "What do I need to do before the crew arrives?"
     - "What happens if something gets damaged during the project?"
   - These are the exact questions homeowners type into Google before calling. Answering them on the page captures long-tail search traffic and reduces the time a prospect spends uncertain.

10. **Final CTA section**
    - H2: "Ready to See What Your Yard Could Look Like?"
    - Short form: name, phone, email, "Describe your project (optional)"
    - Below form: "We respond within [X] hours. No spam, no pressure."

11. **Footer**
    - License number
    - Insurance statement ("Fully insured — certificate available on request")
    - Links: Home, Services, Gallery, About, Contact, Privacy
    - Google review link
    - Social links (Instagram + Facebook only — this niche is visual)

---

## Mobile-first requirements

- Sticky mobile CTA bar: "Call Now" + "Get a Quote" — persists on scroll
- Gallery must be swipeable on mobile, not just a grid
- Hero CTA buttons must be thumb-sized (min 48px height)
- Phone number must be a tap-to-call link throughout

---

## Copy tone rules for this niche

- First-person plural ("we build", "our crew") — sounds like a real team, not a solo operator
- Specific nouns, not vague nouns: "natural stone patio" not "outdoor living space"
- Homeowner language: "your yard", "your vision", "your budget" — not "our services" and "our solutions"
- No buzzwords: no "seamless", "robust", "leverage", "stunning" (overused in competitor sites)
- One sentence answers — homeowners are scanning, not reading

---

## Photography brief for client onboarding

Before building the site, request from the client:
1. 10-15 finished project photos (exterior, daylight, well-composed — no blurry phone shots)
2. 3-5 before/after pairs with both photos taken from the same angle
3. Owner headshot (outdoor, natural light preferred)
4. Crew in action shot (1-2 photos of team working on a job)
5. Any press features, awards, or association logos (NALP, PLANET, BBB, local chamber)

If the client has no photos: pause and require a 1-hour site-documentation shoot before proceeding. A landscaping site without real photos cannot perform in this niche.

---

## Key differentiators to build into every site

1. Deposit protection section (non-negotiable — always included)
2. Filterable project gallery with cost ranges and labels
3. Split buyer journey (maintenance vs design/install) from the hero section
4. Named crew profiles with real photos
5. Local FAQ content targeting the homeowner's exact decision questions
