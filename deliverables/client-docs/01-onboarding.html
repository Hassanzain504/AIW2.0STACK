Why the pages go live gradually

Google treats a website that publishes fifty-seven pages in one month as spam. The same fifty-seven pages, released two or three a week, read as a business that is growing. Same work, same price, better result.

2. What I need from you

In the first week

  • Account access. Google Business Profile, Google Search Console, Google Analytics, website admin, hosting, and domain or DNS. If you are not sure who holds these, tell me early. Tracking them down can take several days on its own.
  • Licence and insurance numbers, and your SAM.gov details including UEI, CAGE code and the NAICS codes you are registered under.
  • Two decisions. Whether you will answer the phone 24/7, and confirmation that you are commercial and specialty rather than residential. Both change what gets built.
  • A business email inbox I can reach. Verification links for the Google profile and every directory listing arrive there.
  • Someone who will answer verification calls. Several platforms verify by automated call or text to your listed number. If nobody picks up, the listing does not go live.

Every week, for as long as we work together

  • Five job photographs. Real work, from your crew, on their phones. This is what replaces the stock photography permanently and it is what unlocks each new town page. A town page without a real job and a real photo from that town will not rank, so this directly controls how fast the remaining pages can be built.

Starting in week one, and this one is only yours

  • The review calls. Business to business reviews come from a phone call, not an automation. The first twenty need to come from you personally, calling clients you have already served. I build the system, write every script and template, and handle every reply. The calls are yours, and they are the single biggest factor in how fast the Google Maps listing moves.

Before anything changes, I record a baseline: your current rankings, your current visibility, your review counts, and your competitors' figures. At the end of the thirty days you get the same measurements again, side by side.

Call tracking is installed in the first week, which means from month two onwards you will be able to see, for the first time, exactly where each enquiry came from.

5. Timing expectations, honestly

  • Technical and speed fixes show up within days.
  • Google Business Profile improvements typically start moving in three to eight weeks. A brand new profile has to clear verification first, which can take time and is not in anyone's control but Google's.
  • New pages take three to six months to mature in search results.
  • Reviews compound. They are slow at first and then they are the thing that carries everything else.

I will not promise you a ranking position, a number of leads, or a revenue figure. Those depend on Google's timing and on the reviews and photographs only you can supply. What I do guarantee is set out in the terms below.
