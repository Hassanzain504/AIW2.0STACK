## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Project gallery with pool type, features, and investment range** (pool buyers are imagining a specific space -- lap pool vs freeform vs spa combo -- and they are comparing budgets; a labeled gallery with investment ranges converts the research stage into a call)
2. **Build process walkthrough with timeline expectations** (a pool build takes 8-16 weeks; homeowners who don't understand the timeline panic and blame the contractor; a plain "here is what happens and when" section removes the #1 source of mid-project reviews)
3. **Named designer or project lead with credentials** (a $60k-$150k pool is a relationship purchase -- homeowners want to know who they are going to be in contact with for 3 months before they sign)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
