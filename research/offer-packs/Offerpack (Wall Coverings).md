OFFER PACK, Wallpaper Installation and Wall Covering Services

"I build websites for wallpaper installers that help homeowners and interior designers find the right hanger before their renovation schedule stalls."

## One-line public offer

"I build websites for wallpaper installers that turn renovation browsers into booked installs."

(For IG bio variant: "Websites for wallpaper installers. Get on the designer's shortlist.")

---

## Stage of business

**Stage: No portfolio**

The correct offer shape is: "I'll design your homepage upfront, for free. If you love it, you pay. If not, you walk. No invoice, no awkward follow-up."

---

## Offer mechanics

**Risk reversal:** Free homepage design upfront. Paid on approval only.

**Price anchor (3-tier, they buy the middle):**

| Tier | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Homepage only, no SEO, 2 revision rounds | $800 |
| Growth (recommend this) | Full 5-page site, basic local SEO, project gallery organized by room type and wallpaper style, mobile-optimized | $1,800 |
| Pro | Full site + GBP optimization + 3-month SEO content plan + automation setup (quote request form + auto-reply with measurement checklist) | $3,500 |

**Payment terms (Stage 1):** Free design upfront. On approval: 50% deposit, 50% on launch.

**The Starbucks guarantee:** "If I waste your time, I'll send you a $25 Starbucks gift card."

---

## Customer language to echo in every DM

1. "I spent a lot on this wallpaper -- I need someone who won't ruin it."
2. "The walls are old and uneven -- is that a problem?"
3. "How long does it take to paper one room?"
4. "Do you remove the old wallpaper too or just install new?"
5. "I have a designer providing the paper -- do you work with client-supplied materials?"
6. "Can you handle textured or grasscloth wallpaper?"
7. "I need it done before the furniture arrives."
8. "My designer recommended I hire a professional -- what's the price per roll or per room?"

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Room-type gallery with close-up seam photos** (homeowners who paid for premium wallpaper are anxious about pattern matching and seam quality -- close-up finished photos answer that concern without a word)
2. **Specialty wallcovering experience** (grasscloth, fabric, and peel-and-stick are different skills; a contractor who lists specific materials they work with signals expertise and filters out mismatched jobs)
3. **Trade and designer referral relationships** (being a named preferred installer for a local design firm is the fastest credibility signal in this market -- even mentioning that designers refer you builds trust)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.
