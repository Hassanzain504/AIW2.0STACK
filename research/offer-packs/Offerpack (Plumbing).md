## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Upfront flat-rate pricing statement** (plumbing has a worse reputation for surprise bills than almost any other trade; one sentence saying the homeowner gets a written price before anything starts removes the #1 objection)
2. **Response time commitment** (a burst pipe is not a scheduled appointment; the contractor who shows a response time in their hero wins before the others are even called)
3. **License number visible in the hero or trust bar** (state plumbing licenses are required everywhere; showing the number instead of just claiming "licensed" signals legitimacy and filters out the fly-by-night competition)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
