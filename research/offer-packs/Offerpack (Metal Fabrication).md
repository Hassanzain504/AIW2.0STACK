| Tier | What they get | Price |

## Customer language to echo in every DM

1. "I needed someone who could actually fabricate custom, not just install off-the-shelf."
2. "I wanted a gate that matched the style of the house exactly."
3. "The railing design was something I had in my head. They made it happen."
4. "I looked at three shops before I found one that could show me their past work."
5. "We needed someone who works with structural steel, not just decorative."
6. "My contractor recommended them but I still went and looked at their website first."
7. "Lead time mattered. We were already behind on the project."
8. "They showed me a sample weld before I committed. That made the difference."

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Project gallery organized by type** (a builder looking for structural steel and a homeowner looking for a decorative stair railing are different buyers, a sorted gallery helps each one self-qualify fast)
2. **Custom fabrication positioning** (buyers fear ordering something that won't fit, showing your design-to-install process reduces that fear)
3. **Lead time and process transparency** (construction timelines are tight, showing your typical turnaround and process builds confidence before they even call)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
