# Ranking Plan: Black Ops Cleaning Company

Companion to `blackopscleaningco-audit.md`. The audit says what is broken. This says what to
rank for, in what order, and why.

Date: 2026-08-21

---

## Where they actually stand

Measured, not estimated:

| | Black Ops | Clean Advantage | Tri State Restorations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indexed pages | 4 | ~151 | ~618 |
| Google reviews | unknown, likely near zero | established | established |
| Angi reviews | 0 | - | - |
| Facebook reviews | 0 | - | - |
| Years of domain history | since 2024 | long | long |

The DMV is one of the most competitive service markets in the United States. Rockville alone
has a full page of established commercial cleaning firms with a decade of history and hundreds
of reviews between them.

A four page site with zero reviews cannot win "commercial cleaning washington dc". Not in six
months, not with any budget that this business can afford. Anyone who promises that is selling
something.

So the plan is not to fight there first.

## The governing rule

Pick fights you can win, bank the revenue, use the revenue and the reviews to fight bigger
fights later.

There are three separate lanes here, with different competitors, different timelines and
different tactics. They run in parallel, but they are not equally urgent.

## Lane A. Map pack in the home ring (Days 0 to 90)

**Target:** top 3 in the Google map pack for commercial cleaning and janitorial terms in
Rockville, then the ring around it: Gaithersburg, North Bethesda, Bethesda, Silver Spring,
Potomac, Derwood, Kensington.

**Why this first.** Proximity is one of the strongest inputs into map pack ordering, and their
pin is in Rockville. This is the one place where geography is already working for them. Every
mile outward it gets harder. Win the centre before reaching outward.

**Why it is winnable.** Map pack ranking is far more about profile completeness, category
accuracy and review velocity than about domain authority or page count. A brand new business
with an excellent profile and 30 real reviews beats a ten year old business with a half-built
profile and 12 reviews. Those businesses exist in every market, including this one.

**Keyword targets:**

```
commercial cleaning rockville md
janitorial services rockville md
office cleaning rockville md
commercial cleaning montgomery county
janitorial services gaithersburg md
office cleaning bethesda md
commercial cleaning silver spring md
post construction cleaning rockville
```

**What it requires, in order:**
1. GBP rebuilt completely (audit section 1)
2. Reviews, starting immediately, and this is the whole game (see the review section below)
3. NAP consistency and citation cleanup, including the duplicate Facebook page
4. A real page per city in the ring, one per week
5. Weekly GBP posts and weekly real photos, forever

**Realistic timeline:** movement in 3 to 8 weeks, top 3 in Rockville in 3 to 6 months, and only
if the reviews happen. Without reviews this lane stalls no matter what else is done.

## Lane B. Specialty and emergency work (Days 30 to 180)

**This is the fastest revenue in the entire plan and it is currently being ignored.**

**Target:** biohazard, trauma, crime scene, unattended death, hoarding, sewage backup and
decontamination terms across Montgomery County, DC and Northern Virginia.

**Why this lane is different.** The competitor set is not two hundred local cleaning companies.
It is roughly six to ten regional firms: Tri State Restorations, SI Restoration, Crime Scene
Inc, Bio-One of DC, Paul Davis, Purofirst. Most of them are general restoration companies where
biohazard is one service line among twenty. Black Ops can out-specialise them on content
because for Black Ops it is a core service, not a footnote.

**Why the economics are better:**
- Search intent is urgent. Nobody researches unattended death cleanup for three weeks.
- Price sensitivity is near zero. The buyer wants it handled today.
- Margins are several times higher than office janitorial.
- Volume is lower, but one job can equal months of janitorial revenue.
- Many of these jobs are insurance-paid, so the buyer is not spending their own money.

**Keyword targets:**

```
biohazard cleanup rockville md
biohazard cleanup montgomery county
crime scene cleanup maryland
crime scene cleanup washington dc
unattended death cleanup maryland
trauma scene cleanup montgomery county
hoarding cleanup rockville md
hoarding cleanup maryland
sewage backup cleanup rockville
sewage cleanup montgomery county
blood cleanup services maryland
decomposition odor removal maryland
```

**What it requires:**
1. A dedicated page per specialty service, written properly. These pages need care: the reader
   is in the worst week of their life. Calm, factual, no marketing language, no stock photos,
   clear on discretion, insurance and timelines.
2. City variants for the highest volume terms only
3. **A 24/7 answered phone line.** This lane does not work with 9 to 5 hours. Emergency callers
   call the next number after two rings. This is a business decision that has to be made before
   the pages are built, or the traffic converts at near zero.
4. Content that answers what people actually search at 2am: does insurance cover it, how long
   does it take, will neighbours know, what does it cost, who do I call first, do police clean
   it up (they do not).
5. Relationships that also produce links and referrals: funeral homes, property managers,
   landlords, police departments, victim services, insurance adjusters, plumbers who hit sewage.

**Realistic timeline:** first rankings in 6 to 12 weeks. This is faster than Lane A because the
competition is thinner and the content bar is genuinely low. Most competitor pages on these
terms are two paragraphs and a phone number.

## Lane C. Commercial, medical, government contracts (Days 90 to 365)

**Target:** the higher value recurring contracts. Medical and healthcare facilities, government
and federal buildings, larger commercial properties.

This lane is only partly an SEO problem, and the non-SEO half is bigger.

**The SEO half:**

```
medical office cleaning rockville md
healthcare facility cleaning maryland
medical facility cleaning bethesda
GBAC certified disinfection maryland
infection control cleaning services maryland
government facility cleaning washington dc
federal building janitorial services dc
veteran owned cleaning company washington dc
```

Their GBAC and ISSA CMI certifications are real and uncommon among small firms. That belongs on
a dedicated infection control and healthcare cleaning page, not buried in a shared services
page. Very few small competitors can write that page truthfully.

**The non-SEO half, which matters more.**

The business is veteran-owned and the site says formal certification is being pursued. In the
DMV, sitting on top of the largest concentration of federal facilities in the country, SDVOSB
or VOSB certification is not a trust badge. It is a procurement channel. Federal set-aside
contracts are legally restricted to certified firms, which means the competition is not two
hundred cleaning companies, it is the handful of certified ones that also do this work.

Action items that are not SEO but will out-earn the SEO:
1. Finish the SBA veteran-owned certification. Treat it as the highest priority business task.
2. Register in SAM.gov.
3. Get a CAGE code and the relevant NAICS codes registered (janitorial is 561720).
4. Look at GSA schedules and subcontracting to existing prime contractors.
5. Build one page targeting government facility contracting, which also serves as the landing
   page for procurement officers who search the company name after seeing a bid.

**Realistic timeline:** 6 to 12 months, largest payoff of anything in this document.

## The review problem, which is the actual bottleneck

Everything in Lane A and most of Lane B is capped by this. Zero reviews on Angi, zero on
Facebook, and almost certainly near zero on Google.

**Do not set a round number target. Get the real one.** Search each target keyword in each
target city, record the review count of the three businesses currently in the map pack, and
that is the number. Match the third one within six months.

**Operating rules:**
- Velocity matters more than total. A steady 5 to 10 a month beats 40 in a week, and the burst
  looks manipulated and can get filtered.
- B2B review generation is a phone call, not an automation. The founder personally calls every
  past and current client, one at a time, over four to six weeks. Nobody else can do this.
- For specialty and trauma work, ask carefully or not at all. Many of those clients will not
  want to be publicly associated with the job. Never pressure them.
- Never gate (asking only happy customers violates policy), never buy, never incentivise.
- Reply to every review within 24 hours, naming the service and the city naturally.
- Diversify: Google first, then Facebook, BBB, Angi, and industry directories.

**If the founder will not make these calls, this plan caps out at Lane B and the plan should
say so honestly rather than pretending otherwise.**

## Build order for the first 90 days

### Weeks 1 to 2. Foundation and damage control
- Take full access, capture the baseline, install call tracking (audit section 0)
- Remove the CDC, EPA and OSHA logos
- Compress every image, convert to WebP, get the homepage under 1 MB
- Fix H1s on all four pages, rewrite the four title tags and meta descriptions
- Resolve the residential vs commercial positioning contradiction, permanently
- Add LocalBusiness schema with phone, address, areaServed, geo, hours
- Rebuild the GBP: category, service area, services, products, description, attributes, photos
- Merge the duplicate Facebook page
- Founder starts calling past clients for reviews
- **Decide the 24/7 phone question.** Everything in Lane B depends on this answer.

### Weeks 3 to 6. The pages that make money
Two to three real pages a week, in this order:

1. Biohazard remediation
2. Crime scene and trauma cleanup
3. Unattended death cleanup
4. Sewage backup and decontamination
5. Hoarding cleanup
6. Commercial janitorial / office cleaning
7. Medical and healthcare facility cleaning
8. Government and federal facility cleaning

Specialty pages first, deliberately. They are the fastest to rank, the highest margin, and the
least contested.

  Potomac, Washington DC, Arlington, Alexandria

- First outreach round: funeral homes, property managers, plumbers, insurance adjusters

## Content depth, so the page count is realistic

The gap is 4 pages against 151 and 618. Closing it is not a weekend job and it must not be a
bulk generation job, which would get the whole site treated as thin.

Realistic build: 13 service pages, 10 city pages, 8 to 12 specialty city variants, plus a
bottom-of-funnel blog at 2 to 4 posts a month. That is roughly 35 to 40 real pages in six
months at a sustainable 2 to 3 a week, then continuing.

Blog priorities, all bottom of funnel:
```
how much does biohazard cleanup cost in maryland
does homeowners insurance cover unattended death cleanup
how long does crime scene cleanup take
who cleans up after a death at home
what does commercial cleaning cost per square foot in maryland
how often should a medical office be professionally cleaned
what is GBAC certification and why it matters
sewage backup in a commercial building, what to do first
how to choose a janitorial contractor for a federal facility
```

Each answers the question directly in the first 40 to 60 words under a question-shaped heading.
That format is what gets pulled into AI answers, which now sit above the organic results.

## What not to spend money on

- Chasing "commercial cleaning washington dc" in year one. Not winnable yet.
- Buying links or guest post packages. In a market this scrutinised it is downside only.
- Bulk generating a hundred city pages. It will get the site filtered.
- Residential cleaning content. Nothing about this business supports it.
- A site rebuild. The current WordPress and LiteSpeed setup is fine. TTFB is 0.28s. The problem
  is images and missing pages, not the platform.
- Any tactic that depends on the client not being asked to do anything. Reviews and job photos
  require them, and there is no substitute.

## How to tell it is working, before rankings move

Rankings are a lagging indicator. Watch these instead:

**Day 30:**
- Homepage under 1 MB, Core Web Vitals green on mobile
- GBP profile 100% complete, weekly posts running
- At least 8 to 10 new Google reviews
- 6 or more real pages live
- Call tracking reporting by source
- Search Console impressions rising, even if clicks have not yet

**Day 60:**
- Ranking in the top 10 for at least 3 specialty terms
- Map pack appearances in Rockville for at least one core term
- 20 or more reviews
- 15 or more pages live
- First inbound calls attributable to organic or map pack

**Day 90:**
- Top 3 map pack in Rockville for at least one core term
- Page 1 for several specialty terms
- 30 or more reviews with a steady monthly rhythm
- 25 or more pages live
- A measurable lead count from organic, with a cost per lead to compare against paid

If Day 30 is missed, the cause is almost always that the client did not supply reviews or
photos. Name it early rather than at month three.

## The one decision that gates everything

The business has to choose what it is. Right now the metadata says residential, the content says
commercial, the certifications say healthcare and infection control, and the founder's story
says government contracting. Those are four different companies with four different keyword
sets, four different buyers and four different price points.

The evidence points to one answer: a certified specialty remediation and facility cleaning firm,
veteran-owned, in the highest concentration of federal facilities in the country. That is a
narrower business than the site currently claims, and a far more valuable one.

Nothing in this plan works properly until that is decided and every page reflects it.
