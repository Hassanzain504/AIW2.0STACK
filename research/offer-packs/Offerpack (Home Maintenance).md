---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Named handyman with background check and insurance statement** (handyman is the highest-trust single-person service -- the homeowner is often not home and the handyman has full access; a name, photo, and background-check badge converts the homeowner who is uncomfortable with a stranger in their house)
2. **Service list with honest limitations** (handymen who say "we do everything" are trusted less than ones who say "here's what we do well, here's what we don't do" -- specificity signals competence)
3. **Hourly rate or day-rate visible on the homepage** (handyman pricing is the #1 question before booking; the service that shows a rate range removes the anxiety of an unknown bill and converts the homeowner who is deciding between calling and doing it themselves)
