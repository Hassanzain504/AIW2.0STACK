## Part 2. The three models for town pages

This is the decision that sets the total page count.

### Model A: one page per town
One generic page per town covering the core service. **17 to 23 pages.**
Simple, safe, manageable. Ranks for "commercial cleaning [town]".

### Model B: every service in every town
11 services multiplied by 20 towns. **220 pages.**
This is what large multi-state operators do. For a business this size it is the wrong move.
Most of it would be near-identical text, Google treats it as doorway content, and it can drag
the whole site down.

### Model C: hybrid, and this is the right one
- One page per town for the core commercial service
- Then service-plus-town pages **only** for the highest value services in the highest value
  towns

That means biohazard, unattended death, hoarding and medical facility cleaning get their own
town variants in the six or seven towns where they actually matter. Everything else stays at
one page per town.

## Part 4. Phasing, so the number is not frightening

Never bulk publish. Two to three real pages a week, forever.

| Phase | When | Pages | Running total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint | Weeks 1 to 8 | 10 service + 4 town | 14 |
| Phase 2 | Months 3 to 8 | 3 service + 8 town + 6 supporting | 31 |
| Phase 3 | Months 9 to 18 | 5 town + 19 service-plus-town + 2 supporting | 57 |

At two pages a month inside the retainer, phases 2 and 3 take roughly 18 months. That is normal.
Clean Method's 500 pages were not built in a quarter either.

## Part 5. The cap nobody mentions

**The real limit is not budget or time. It is whether the client has done a job in that town.**

Every town page needs a real job completed there, a real photograph from it, and a real local
detail. If you cannot supply those, the page fails the swap test, reads as filler, and will not
rank. Building it anyway makes the site worse, not better.

So the practical sequence is:
1. Build town pages where jobs already exist
2. As new jobs land in new towns, the crew photographs them
3. That job becomes the next town page

This is why the photo routine matters more than it sounds. It is not decoration. It is the raw
material that unlocks each new town page, and without it the page count stalls at whatever the
client can currently evidence.

**Realistic near-term ceiling:** if Black Ops has genuinely worked in only five or six towns so
far, build those five or six properly and stop. Six real town pages beat seventeen invented
ones, every time.
