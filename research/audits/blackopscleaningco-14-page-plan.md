# 14 Page Build Plan: Black Ops Cleaning

Build spec for the Foundation Sprint. Ten service pages and four town pages.
Build in the order given. Specialty first, because it is the thinnest competition and the
fastest to rank.

Base: Rockville, MD 20852. Service area: Montgomery County, DC, Northern Virginia.
Certifications to use throughout: ISSA CMI, GBAC trained, veteran-owned, insured.
Do not use CDC, EPA or OSHA logos anywhere.

---

## Cannibalisation rule, read this first

The service page and the town page must not fight each other.

- **Service pages target the service at region level.** "Commercial Cleaning Services | DMV"
- **Town pages target service plus town.** "Commercial Cleaning Rockville MD"

Never give two pages the same title pattern. The current site already has this problem between
the homepage and /services/, and it is why neither ranks.

---

## Global page furniture

Every page in this list gets all of the following. Build it once as an Elementor template and
reuse it.

**Above the fold**
- One H1, service and location, plain language
- Phone as a `tel:` link, visible without scrolling
- Short form: name, phone, email, service, message
- One-line promise

**Trust bar, directly under the fold**
```
Veteran-Owned  |  ISSA CMI Certified  |  GBAC Trained  |  Fully Insured  |  Serving the DMV
```

**Footer on every page**
- NAP exactly: Black Ops Cleaning Company LLC, 121 Talbott St, Rockville, MD 20852, 240-221-1914
- The real Google Business Profile link, not the city-of-Rockville link that is there now
- Service area list

**Schema on every page:** `LocalBusiness` (site-wide), `Service` (service pages),
`FAQPage` (all), `BreadcrumbList`

# PART C. TOWN PAGES

Four in the sprint. Then two per month in the retainer.

**The swap test:** if you can change the town name and the page still reads fine, it is a
doorway page and it will not rank. Every town page needs a real job done in that town, a real
photo from it, and a real local detail. If the client cannot supply those for a town yet, build
a different town first.

**Shared structure for all four**
1. H1: Commercial Cleaning in [Town], MD
2. Above fold: phone, form, drive time from Rockville
3. H2: Serving [Town] businesses. Drive time, response time, which neighbourhoods and
   business districts.
4. H2: A recent job in [Town]. Real client type, real work, real photo.
5. H2: Services we provide in [Town]. Linked to the ten service pages.
6. H2: [Town] specifics. Building stock, business types, local conditions, anything true and
   particular to that town.
7. H2: What [Town] clients say. A review from a customer in that town.
8. H2: Emergency and specialty coverage in [Town]. Links to the specialty cluster.
9. H2: FAQ, 5 to 6 questions, town specific
10. Map, service area statement, final CTA

**Words:** 700 to 1,000 each. Do not pad.

# Before you publish any page

- One H1 only, and it is not a tagline
- Title unique, under 60 characters, no cannibalisation with another page
- Meta unique, under 155 characters, matches what the page actually offers
- Every image is a real photo, WebP, under 200 KB, with real alt text
- `Service` and `FAQPage` schema present and validating in the Rich Results Test
- Internal links to related services and to the relevant town pages
- Phone as a `tel:` link above the fold
- Form tested with a live submission
- Read it once as the buyer, not as the builder. Would you call?

# Client inputs needed before the build starts

Chase these in week 1 or the build stalls in week 3.

- Real job photos, per service and per town
