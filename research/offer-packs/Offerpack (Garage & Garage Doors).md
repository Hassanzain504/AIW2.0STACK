## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Same-day response badge with a specific window** ("we're there within 3 hours" converts better than "same-day service" -- specificity signals reliability)
2. **Upfront pricing statement** (garage door repairs have a reputation for bait-and-switch pricing; one sentence saying the homeowner gets a price before anything starts removes the #1 objection)
3. **Before/after gallery of door replacements** (a new door changes curb appeal dramatically -- homeowners replacing a door are making an aesthetic decision too, not just a functional one)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
