# Hero Composition - Landscaping Niche

## Required composition

The hero uses a full-bleed background photo with a dark overlay and centered content.

### Image requirements

- Orientation: landscape, 16:9 or wider
- Minimum size: 1500x900px
- Subject: finished outdoor project (patio, full yard, garden transformation)
- Lighting: exterior daylight, morning or golden hour preferred
- Overlay: rgba(0,0,0,0.35) to rgba(0,0,0,0.45) depending on photo brightness
- No stock photos

### Content layout

- H1: centered, Playfair Display 700, white, 48px desktop / 36px mobile
- Subhead: centered, Inter 400, white/90 opacity, 18px
- Phone number: inline in subhead as tap-to-call link
- CTAs: two buttons, side by side on sm+ / stacked on mobile
  - Primary: "See Our Work" (accent green background)
  - Secondary: "Get a Free Estimate" (white border, transparent background)
- Trust bar: below CTAs, 4 items in a flex row, white/80 opacity text

### Above-fold requirement

The full hero section including trust bar must be visible above 900px desktop fold without scrolling.

### Dark overlay calibration

- Bright daytime photo (grass in sun): overlay /45 or /50
- Golden hour photo (warm, lower contrast): overlay /35 or /40
- Default: /40
