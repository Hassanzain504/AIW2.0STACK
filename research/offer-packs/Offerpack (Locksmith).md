## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Technician photo and license number** (the locksmith industry has a well-known scam problem; showing a real face and a state license number is the single fastest trust builder)
2. **Upfront pricing display** (homeowners fear the bait-and-switch quote; a visible price range by service type stops the anxiety before it starts)
3. **Response time guarantee** (a specific time commitment -- "we arrive in 30 minutes or less in [city]" -- converts better than any generic headline)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
