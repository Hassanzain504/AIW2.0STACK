## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Named cleaner profiles with background check statement** (cleaning is the highest-trust home service -- the homeowner is giving a stranger access to their home and their belongings; a named photo and background-check badge converts better than any testimonial)
2. **Recurring plan with the same cleaner every visit** (the #1 difference between a booking and a long-term client is continuity -- homeowners who know they will have the same person every time book recurring plans; competitors rarely mention this on the homepage)
3. **Satisfaction re-clean guarantee** (the #1 complaint in negative cleaning reviews is "missed areas" -- a plain 24-hour re-clean guarantee removes the risk of trying a new service)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
