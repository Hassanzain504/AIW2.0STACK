## Stage of business

**Stage: No portfolio**

Hassan has 4 years of freelance web dev experience but no custom home builder-specific case studies and no existing client in this niche. The correct offer shape is:

"I'll design your homepage upfront, for free. If you love it, you pay. If not, you walk. No invoice, no awkward follow-up."

This removes all risk from the prospect's side. The first 1-2 clients are bought with time, not discounted on price. Once one client is live and producing results, move to Stage 2.

---

## Offer mechanics

**Risk reversal:** Free homepage design upfront. Paid on approval only. First client pays nothing until they see the design and say yes.

**Price anchor (3-tier, they buy the middle):**

| Tier | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Homepage only, no SEO, 2 revision rounds | $800 |
| Growth (recommend this) | Full 5-page site, basic local SEO, completed home gallery with specs, build process timeline, mobile-optimized | $1,800 |
| Pro | Full site + GBP optimization + 3-month SEO content plan + automation setup (consultation booking form + auto-reply) | $3,500 |

**Payment terms (Stage 1):** Free design upfront. On approval: 50% deposit, 50% on launch. After first case study: 30% deposit, 70% on launch.

**The Starbucks guarantee:** "If I waste your time, I'll send you a $25 Starbucks gift card. At least let me show you where I see the missed opportunities."

## Customer language to echo in every DM

Pull these phrases verbatim from custom home builder review patterns. Use them in your DMs and copy:

1. "they listened to exactly what we wanted"
2. "kept us informed at every stage of the build"
3. "delivered on time and on budget"
4. "felt like they cared about our home as much as we did"
5. "transparent about every cost from day one"
6. "we would build with them again without question"
7. "the quality of the finishes exceeded our expectations"
8. "made the whole process less stressful than we expected"

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Completed home gallery with square footage and key features listed** (buyers need to see finished homes at a similar scale and style to theirs before they trust a builder with a six-figure contract)
2. **Detailed build process timeline with communication milestones** (the fear of a build going over schedule and over budget without warning is the top objection, and showing the communication structure removes it)
3. **Named builder profile with years in business and number of homes completed** (a custom home is the largest purchase most people make, and they want to know who is running the job, not just the company name)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
