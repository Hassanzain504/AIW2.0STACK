## Customer language to echo in every DM

1. "the kitchen looks completely different"
2. "helped us pick a style that matched the rest of the house"
3. "the installation was clean -- no gaps, no visible screws"
4. "gave us options at different price points before we decided"
5. "the countertop seams are invisible"
6. "finished on the timeline they gave us"
7. "the quality is way above what the big box store offered"
8. "worth every dollar for the transformation"

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Material and style gallery with investment ranges** (cabinet and countertop buyers are making a style decision and a budget decision at the same time -- a gallery labeled by style, material, and cost range converts the homeowner who is still comparing options)
2. **"Local vs big-box" positioning statement** (Home Depot and IKEA installs are the direct competition; the local company that explicitly says "you work with the same person from the design through the install" wins the homeowner who has heard big-box horror stories)
3. **Installation quality photos showing details** (gaps, misaligned doors, and visible hardware are the #1 complaints -- close-up photos of clean installs showing tight seams and level doors convert the homeowner who cares about craftsmanship)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.
