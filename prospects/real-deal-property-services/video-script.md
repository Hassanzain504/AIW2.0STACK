# Bryan, Real Deal Property Services: 2-minute video script

**Record with:** Loom (screen plus your face in the corner).
**Open before recording:** `prospects/real-deal-property-services/index.html` in Chrome, full screen.
**Have ready:** 2 or 3 photos of a junk pile or messy yard saved on your computer. Do not use screenshots.
**Length:** about 2 minutes. Talk like you are explaining it to a friend. Do not read word for word.

---

## 0:00 to 0:15. Open (page visible, scrolled to top)

"Hey Bryan, Hassan here. Thanks for being open to this. You mentioned quote requests come straight to you, so I built this around that. It is a page where the customer does the legwork and you price it from your phone."

## 0:15 to 0:35. The problem (stay on the top section)

"Right now, if someone in Ridgecrest wants a price on a pile of junk or a yard, you either play phone tag or drive out to look at it. That is gas and an hour gone, and some of those people never book. This page is meant to cut out that drive."

## 0:35 to 1:20. Walk through as the customer (fill it in live)

1. Click **Junk removal**. "First they pick what they need. Junk, yard, or both."
2. Click **Take or add photos** and add 2 or 3 photos. "Then they snap a few photos. On a phone this opens the camera, so it takes ten seconds."
3. Pick a day and **Morning**. "They pick the day that works for them."
4. Type a name, number, and cross streets. "Name, number, and where it is. That is it. Four steps, no long form."
5. Click **Send to Bryan**.

## 1:20 to 1:45. What lands on your phone (point at the phone on the right)

"And this is what you get. One text with everything: what they need, where, when, and the photos right under it. You look at the pile, and you text back a price."

Click **Send price**.

"They get your price by text, and nothing is booked until they say yes."

"On your end there is nothing to install. No app, no new login. It comes to the phone you already use, and you reply like any normal text."

## 1:45 to 2:00. Close

"This one is a demo with your name on it. The real version would sit on its own link you can put on Facebook, Google, or your truck. If it looks useful, I am happy to set it up for you. Let me know what you think."

---

## Notes

- If he asks the price, do not quote it in the video. Save it for the call.
- The $275 in the demo reply is only an example. If he asks, say "That is just a placeholder. You set your own price every time."
- After recording, check that the video is under 2:15 before sending.
