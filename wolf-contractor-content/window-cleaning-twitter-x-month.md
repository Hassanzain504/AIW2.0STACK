## How X is different from LinkedIn (read this first)

X is not LinkedIn with a smaller word count. The rhythm is different. This plan is built for how X actually works.

- The first tweet is everything. If it does not stop the scroll, nothing after it matters.
- One idea per tweet. Short lines. White space.
- Post more often. On X, 1 to 3 times a day per account is normal, not spammy.
- Threads are how you go deep. Singles are how you stay present.
- Hashtags: use 0 to 2, and only at the end. On X they do almost nothing for reach and too many look like spam. #WindowCleaning is the only one worth using regularly. Most posts use none.
- Voice is faster and more direct than LinkedIn. Same brand rules: no emojis, no em-dashes, calm and plain.
- Reply-bait works. End some posts with a question so people reply. Replies feed the algorithm harder than likes.
- Engage back. Reply to every reply for the first 2 hours. Quote-tweet good replies.
