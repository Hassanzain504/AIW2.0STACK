## Who Owns the SERPs

For "HVAC service near me," "HVAC replacement near me," and "best HVAC contractor near me":
- **bbb.org/near-me/heating-and-air-conditioning** — ranks for both navigational and transactional
- **angi.com/nearme/hvac-companies** — Angi has multiple HVAC SERPs
- **thumbtack.com/k/hvac-contractors/near-me** — category pages
- **yelp.com/nearme/hvac-contractors** — review aggregator
- **Manufacturer finders:** lennox.com, carrier.com, bryant.com, daikincomfort.com — rank because homeowners trust brand names as quality proxies
- **homedepot.com/services/c/hvac-installation** and **lowes.com** — home improvement retail capture replacement intent

For cost queries ("AC repair cost"):
- **reddit.com/r/hvacadvice** — community-sourced cost benchmarks rank above contractor pages
- **homedepot.com/services/c/cost-repair-service-hvac** — big box cost pages
- **walkerbrosac.com, trane.com** — local contractor blog posts that rank for national cost queries

Individual contractor sites rarely rank for bare "near me" queries. They rank for:
- City + service: "AC repair [city]," "furnace installation [city]"
- Neighborhood queries: lower competition, high local relevance
- Long-tail research: "how long does an HVAC system last," "repair vs replace HVAC"

## Secondary Keyword Clusters

**Cost and research intent:**
- AC repair cost [city/state]
- HVAC replacement cost
- average cost to replace HVAC system
- how much does a new AC unit cost

Reddit and national cost sites dominate. Local contractor with a genuine cost guide ("What HVAC Replacement Costs in [City] in 2026 — Based on Our Last 200 Installs") can rank for local versions.

**Decision-stage content:**
- repair vs replace HVAC
- how long does an HVAC system last
- HVAC $5000 rule explained
- when should I replace my AC unit

Low competition, high-intent. Homeowners in the decision crossroads search these before calling a contractor. A contractor who answers these questions ranks and gets the call.

**Brand-specific:**
- Carrier AC installation [city]
- Lennox furnace replacement [city]
- Trane HVAC service [city]

Homeowners who have done brand research and prefer a specific manufacturer search this way. Manufacturer authorization makes these queries addressable.

## Schema Markup Recommendations

1. **LocalBusiness schema** — NAP, hours, service area, rating. Required.
2. **Service schema** — One per service page (AC Repair, Furnace Repair, HVAC Installation, Emergency, Maintenance).
3. **FAQPage schema** — For the $5,000 rule and other PAA-matching FAQ content.
4. **Review/AggregateRating schema** — Star display in organic results; increases CTR.
5. **Offer schema** — For financing pages and maintenance plan pricing.

---

## Content Gaps

1. **The $5,000 rule explained locally.** "What is the $5,000 rule for HVAC?" appears in every SERP page analyzed. It is the most searched HVAC decision question. No contractor site in the corpus has a dedicated, honest page answering this with local cost data. This is the highest-value content gap in the niche.

2. **Honest brand comparison content.** "What are the top 3 AC brands?" appears repeatedly. A contractor who publishes "Carrier vs Trane vs Lennox: What We Tell Our [City] Customers" with honest tradeoffs ranks for the brand comparison query and builds authority before the competitor gets to the phone.

3. **Local replacement cost guides.** National averages dominate cost queries. A contractor's local "What HVAC Replacement Costs in [City] This Year — From Our Last 200 Jobs" page is more credible to a [City] homeowner and targets a lower-competition local variant.

4. **Maintenance plan comparison content.** "Is an HVAC maintenance plan worth it?" has no good contractor-published answer in the SERP. A contractor who answers this honestly (including "here is when it is not worth it") wins trust before the competitor.

## GBP Priority

Same as roofing: "near me" queries are dominated by GBP and aggregators. The website wins by:
- City + service pages targeting specific queries
- GBP fully optimized with all service categories, photos, and Q&A populated
- Review velocity: steady new reviews matter more than a large old batch
