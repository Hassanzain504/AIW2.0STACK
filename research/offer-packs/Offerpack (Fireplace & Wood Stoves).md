## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **NFI certification or equivalent credential displayed prominently** (fireplace installation involves gas lines, chimneys, and carbon monoxide risk, so homeowners want certified installers, not handymen)
2. **Installed project gallery showing completed surrounds and hearths** (homeowners are making both a functional and aesthetic decision, and real installs convert far better than product catalog images)
3. **Safety and warranty explanation page** (the fear of fire or gas leak is the number one unstated objection, and addressing it directly on the site removes the biggest barrier to calling)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
