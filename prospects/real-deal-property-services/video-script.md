# Bryan, Real Deal Property Services: exact 2-minute video script

Har step mein do cheezein hain:
- **KARO:** screen par kya karna hai (Urdu mein)
- **BOLO:** exact alfaaz jo bolne hain (English mein)

Bolne wale alfaaz kul takreeban 300 hain, jo aaram se bolne par 2 minute bante hain.

---

## Recording se pehle (5 minute ki tayyari)

1. Computer par ek folder mein **junk pile ki 3 photos** rakhein (Google Images se "garage junk pile" ya "backyard debris" download kar lein). Screenshots use na karein.
2. `prospects/real-deal-property-services/index.html` ko **Chrome** mein kholein.
3. Browser zoom **90%** kar dein (Ctrl aur minus) taake form aur phone dono ek saath screen par aayein.
4. Page ko **refresh** karein taake form khaali ho.
5. Baaki saare tabs aur notifications band kar dein.
6. **Loom** kholein: "Screen + Camera" chunein, sirf yeh Chrome tab select karein, aur camera ka gol bubble **neeche left corner** mein rakhein.
7. Ek baar poora script zor se parh lein. Phir record karein.

---

## STEP 1: Opening (0:00 se 0:12)

**KARO:** Page top par hai. Kuch click na karein. Camera mein dekh kar muskurayein.

**BOLO:**
> "Hey Bryan, Hassan here. Thanks for saying you're open to this. Here's the page I mentioned, built for Real Deal."

---

## STEP 2: Problem (0:12 se 0:30)

**KARO:** Mouse ko green heading "Snap a photo. Get a price. Pick a day." par le jaayein.

**BOLO:**
> "Right now, when someone in Ridgecrest wants a price on junk or a yard, you either go back and forth on the phone, or you drive out to look at it. That's gas and an hour each time, and some of those people never book. This is meant to cut that drive out."

---

## STEP 3: Job type (0:30 se 0:38)

**KARO:** **"Junk removal"** wale button par click karein.

**BOLO:**
> "So here's what your customer sees. First they pick what they need. Junk, yard, or both."

---

## STEP 4: Photos (0:38 se 0:55)

**KARO:** **"Take or add photos"** wale box par click karein. Folder se 3 photos select karke "Open" dabayein. Photos form mein nazar aayengi.

**BOLO:**
> "Then they snap a few photos of the pile. On a phone, this opens the camera straight away, so it takes about ten seconds. Two or three photos is enough for you to price it."

---

## STEP 5: Day (0:55 se 1:03)

**KARO:** Pehle **Saturday** wale din par click karein, phir **"Morning"** par.

**BOLO:**
> "They pick a day and a time that works for them. Say, Saturday morning."

---

## STEP 6: Contact details (1:03 se 1:15)

**KARO:** Ye teen cheezein type karein:
- Name: `Maria`
- Mobile: `(760) 555-0142`
- Address: `China Lake Blvd and Ridgecrest Blvd`

**BOLO (type karte hue):**
> "Then their name, number, and where it is. That's the whole thing. Four quick steps, no long form."

---

## STEP 7: Send (1:15 se 1:20)

**KARO:** Orange **"Send to Bryan"** button par click karein. Left side par "Sent. Bryan has it." aayega.

**BOLO:**
> "They hit send, and that's it on their end."

---

## STEP 8: Bryan ka phone (1:20 se 1:38)

**KARO:** Mouse ko right side wale phone par le jaayein, pehle text message par, phir photos par.

**BOLO:**
> "And this is what lands on your phone. One text with everything. What they need, where it is, when they want it, and the photos right underneath. You look at the pile, you know your price."

---

## STEP 9: Reply (1:38 se 1:50)

**KARO:** Phone ke neeche **"Send price"** button par click karein. Neela reply bubble aayega.

**BOLO:**
> "You text them back a price, right from your normal phone. Nothing gets booked until they say yes. And there's no app for you to install and nothing new to learn. It works like any other text."

---

## STEP 10: Close (1:50 se 2:05)

**KARO:** Mouse chhod dein. Camera mein dekhein.

**BOLO:**
> "This one's a demo with your name on it. The real one would be a link you can put on Facebook, your Google listing, or a sticker on the truck. If it looks useful, I'm happy to set it up for you. Just reply and let me know what you think."

**KARO:** 2 second ruk kar Loom ka **Stop** dabayein.

---

## Recording ke baad

1. Video ek dafa poori dekhein. 2:15 se lambi ho to dobara record karein.
2. Loom par title rakhein: **"Bryan, quote page for Real Deal"**
3. Link copy karke yeh email bhejein:

> **Subject:** Re: requests that land with you
>
> Hi Bryan,
>
> Here it is: [Loom link]
>
> It shows the customer side first, then what lands on your phone. About 2 minutes.
>
> One question while you watch: on a typical week, roughly how many quotes do you drive out to look at?
>
> Hassan

## Agar galti ho jaaye

- **Koi lafz bhool gaye:** rukein nahi, apne alfaaz mein bol dein. Natural lagna exact alfaaz se zyada zaroori hai.
- **Galat button dab gaya:** page refresh karein aur us step se dobara shuru karein. Loom mein baad mein shuru ka hissa kaat sakte hain.
- **Photos upload nahi huin:** dekhein ke files JPG ya PNG hain.
