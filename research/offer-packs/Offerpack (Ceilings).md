## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Before-and-after photos of ceiling transformations** (ceiling work is entirely visual. a before-and-after of popcorn removal or a coffered ceiling install closes the estimate request faster than any description)
2. **Dust containment and clean-up process description** (popcorn and drywall ceiling work is notoriously messy. homeowners who have heard horror stories about dust in every corner respond to a contractor who addresses it directly)
3. **Asbestos and lead-safe certification mention** (homes built before 1980 often have popcorn ceilings with asbestos. a certified contractor who names this addresses the homeowner's biggest unspoken fear and eliminates all competitors who don't)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
