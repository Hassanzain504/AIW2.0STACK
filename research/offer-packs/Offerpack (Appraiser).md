## Customer language to echo in every DM

1. "She had the report back to me in 48 hours, which saved our closing."
2. "He explained every comparable he used and why."
3. "The value came in right where we expected. No surprises."
4. "She knows this neighborhood better than anyone. Every street."
5. "Licensed, certified, and easy to reach. That's all we ask."
6. "The lender accepted his report first time with no revision requests."
7. "He gave us a realistic number, not one padded to make us feel good."
8. "Fast turnaround and a clean, professional report."

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Credentials display (Certified Residential Appraiser, state license number)** (lenders and agents need to verify credentials before ordering. showing them upfront removes a friction step)
2. **Turnaround time commitment** (closings run on deadlines. a clear "48-72 hour report turnaround" is a direct revenue signal for any agent who sends repeat business)
3. **Local market expertise statement with years and zip codes served** (appraisers win repeat agent business by demonstrating they know the micro-market, not just the county)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
