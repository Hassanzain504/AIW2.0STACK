## Average Ticket (what homeowners pay)

**Lawn care and maintenance:**
- Weekly mowing (residential): $30-$80 per visit depending on lot size
- lawnstarter.com lists from $19; professional full-service typically $50-$80
- Monthly lawn care service: $150-$400/month
- Annual maintenance contract: $1,500-$4,000/year

**Landscape design and installation:**
- Basic front yard update (plants, mulch, edging): $1,000-$3,500
- Mid-level backyard project (sod, irrigation, planting): $5,000-$15,000
- Full backyard transformation (pavers, artificial grass, fire pit, built-in grill): $15,000-$40,000+
- Synthetic grass installation alone: $8-$20 per sqft installed; $5,000-$20,000 per project
- Patio installation: $3,500-$12,000 depending on materials and size

**From SERP PAA data:**

## Revenue Model for the Landscaping Contractor Client

**Two distinct revenue streams:**

**Maintenance (recurring):**
- 50-150 properties per crew
- Average monthly per property: $200-$400
- Revenue per crew per month: $10,000-$60,000 depending on mix
- High predictability; lower margin per job

**Design/installation (project-based):**
- 3-10 projects per month depending on size
- Average project: $8,000-$20,000 for mid-to-large work
- Revenue per month: $24,000-$200,000 depending on project type and volume
- Lower predictability; higher margin per job

**The most valuable contractor to target:**
A landscaping company doing both: maintenance for cash flow plus design/installation for margin. These businesses need a website that serves both buyer personas and converts both inquiry types.

## Lead Economics

**What a design project lead is worth:**
- Average design/installation project: $12,000
- [inferred] Gross margin: 40-50% on installed landscape projects
- Gross profit per project: $4,800-$6,000
- Close rate from website lead: [inferred] 25-35% for design projects (lower than HVAC emergency; higher consideration process)

**What a maintenance lead is worth:**
- Average annual contract: $2,400/year
- [inferred] Gross margin: 45-55% on maintenance
- Gross profit: $1,080-$1,320/year
- Close rate from website lead: [inferred] 35-50% (lower-friction; often booked immediately)
- Lifetime value: 3-5 year average customer relationship = $3,240-$6,600 LTV per maintenance customer

**The design-to-maintenance pipeline:**
A homeowner who hires for a one-off design project often becomes a maintenance customer. One design project at $12,000 can convert to a 3-year maintenance relationship at $2,400/year = $7,200 additional revenue. Total LTV per design client: ~$19,200.

## Selling the Website to the Contractor

**What the contractor cares about:**
- Filling the schedule with design projects (highest margin)
- Reducing no-shows and ghosting after initial inquiry
- Converting the website visitor into a yard assessment appointment
- Recurring maintenance revenue for cash flow stability

**The student's pitch framing:**
A landscaping company doing $500K/year in design/installation work with a 40% margin is generating $200K in gross profit. One additional mid-size project per month at $12,000 adds $144,000 in annual revenue. A website that generates 3-5 design project leads per month at a 30% close rate produces 1-2 additional projects. The website pays for itself in the first project, often in the first month.
