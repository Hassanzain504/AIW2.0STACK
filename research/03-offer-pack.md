## Stage of business

**Stage: No portfolio**

Hassan has 4 years of freelance web dev experience but no landscaping-specific case studies and no existing client in this niche. The correct offer shape is:

"I'll design your homepage upfront, for free. If you love it, you pay. If not, you walk. No invoice, no awkward follow-up."

This removes all risk from the prospect's side. The first 1-2 clients are bought with time, not discounted on price. Once one client is live and producing results, move to Stage 2.

---

## Offer mechanics

**Risk reversal:** Free homepage design upfront. Paid on approval only. First client pays nothing until they see the design and say yes.

**Price anchor (3-tier, they buy the middle):**

| Tier | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Homepage only, no SEO, 2 revision rounds | $800 |
| Growth (recommend this) | Full 5-page site, basic local SEO, gallery, deposit protection section, mobile-optimized | $1,800 |
| Pro | Full site + GBP optimization + 3-month SEO content plan + automation setup (speed-to-lead form + auto-reply) | $3,500 |

**Payment terms (Stage 1):** Free design upfront. On approval: 50% deposit, 50% on launch. After first case study: 30% deposit, 70% on launch.

**The Starbucks guarantee:** "If I waste your time, I'll send you a $25 Starbucks gift card. At least let me show you where I see the missed opportunities."

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

From 05-trust-signals.md:

1. **Before/after project gallery** (filterable, labeled with project type and investment range)
2. **Deposit protection statement** ("Your deposit is protected by a written contract")
3. **Named team profiles with photos** (addresses the "who is coming to my property" anxiety)

These are the three things no competitor agency builds into landscaping sites as standard. Name them by function, not by jargon.

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
