## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Credentials prominently displayed: InterNACHI or ASHI certification, state license, E&O insurance** (homebuyers choosing an inspector independent of their agent want proof of credentials; the inspector who shows them visibly wins the buyer who is doing their own research)
2. **Sample inspection report available to preview** (homebuyers who have never hired an inspector don't know what they're buying; a link to a sample report on the homepage converts the undecided buyer who wants to see what they'll receive)
3. **Same-day or next-day availability statement** (home inspections have hard deadlines -- the contract contingency period is typically 7-10 days; inspectors who clearly state their availability win the calls from buyers who are under time pressure)
