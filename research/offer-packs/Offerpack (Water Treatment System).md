## Customer language to echo in every DM

1. "Our water smells like sulfur and I don't know what to do."
2. "We have hard water and our appliances keep clogging up."
3. "I got the water tested and it came back with high iron -- what system fixes that?"
4. "How much does a whole-house filtration system cost?"
5. "I don't want to keep buying bottled water forever."
6. "My dishes have white spots and my hair feels like straw."
7. "Do you rent systems or only sell them?"
8. "We're on well water -- is that different from city water treatment?"

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Problem-to-solution guide by water issue type** (homeowners who got a bad water test don't know which system they need -- a guide that maps hard water, iron, sulfur, and chlorine to specific solutions positions the contractor as an educator and builds immediate trust)
2. **Free water test offer** (offering a free test removes the biggest barrier to contact -- homeowners who don't know what's in their water have no reason to call without this offer)
3. **Brand partnerships and certifications** (listing recognized brands like Kinetico, EcoWater, or Culligan alongside WQA certification immediately legitimizes the contractor in a market full of door-to-door sales pressure)
