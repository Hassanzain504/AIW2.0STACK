## Offer mechanics

**Risk reversal:** Free homepage design upfront. Paid on approval only.

**Price anchor (3-tier, they buy the middle):**

| Tier | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Homepage only, no SEO, 2 revision rounds | $800 |
| Growth (recommend this) | Full 5-page site, basic local SEO, service menu with fan types and pricing ranges, mobile-optimized | $1,800 |
| Pro | Full site + GBP optimization + 3-month SEO content plan + automation setup (online booking form + auto-reply with appointment confirmation) | $3,500 |

**Payment terms (Stage 1):** Free design upfront. On approval: 50% deposit, 50% on launch.

**The Starbucks guarantee:** "If I waste your time, I'll send you a $25 Starbucks gift card."

## Customer language to echo in every DM

1. "My house gets unbearable in the summer and I hate running AC all day."
2. "How much does ceiling fan installation actually cost?"
3. "Can you swap out my old fan for a new one in the same spot?"
4. "Do I need an electrician or can a handyman do it?"
5. "Will a whole-house fan actually cool things down?"
6. "My ceiling is vaulted -- is that a problem?"
7. "How long does it take to install one fan?"
8. "Can you install multiple fans in the same visit?"

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Licensed electrician credential** (homeowners worried about wiring safety trust a licensed electrician far more than a general handyman for fan installation)
2. **Transparent pricing per fan type** (quoting a range upfront reduces the number of tire-kickers and increases the quality of calls you receive)
3. **Same-day or next-day availability** (fan installation is often driven by immediate discomfort; speed-to-schedule is a major differentiator in this niche)
