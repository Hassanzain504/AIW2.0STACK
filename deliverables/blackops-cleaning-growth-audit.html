03

Google Maps: you are not on it

This is the largest single gap in your marketing, and the fastest one to close.

Our finding: there is almost certainly no Google Business Profile

We checked six ways and found no business listing:

  • The two map links on your website resolve to the city of Rockville and the state of Virginia, not to a business
  • Your site tells Google about Facebook and Instagram but names no Google profile
  • There is no map embedded anywhere, not even on the contact page
  • There is no invitation to review you on Google anywhere
  • Zero reviews on Angi. Zero on Facebook.
  • Across six separate searches, no Maps listing for your company appeared

Every search for a cleaner near Rockville is showing a competitor's map listing. Yours is not ranked low. It is not in the race.

Why the map matters more than the website, at first

  • It is the top of the page. For any "near me" or town-based search, the map block sits above the normal results. Most people never scroll past it.
  • AI has not taken it. AI Overviews are eating clicks from the normal results but barely touch the map. That makes the map more valuable this year than last, not less.
  • Proximity is on your side, here and nowhere else. Distance from the searcher is one of the strongest factors in the map. Your pin is in Rockville. This is the one place geography is already working for you.
  • It moves in weeks, not months. Website rankings take three to six months. A properly built profile starts shifting in three to eight weeks.

The review system, and why it is the whole game

A review does two separate jobs at once, and people usually only think about the second one.

What reviews doEffect
Decide whether you appear at allReview count and, more importantly, how regularly new ones arrive, are among the strongest factors in whether Google shows you in the map block. This is why a new company with a good profile and 30 real reviews beats a ten year old company with a half-built profile and 12.
Decide whether anyone calls youThree companies appear in the map block. A facility manager compares three star ratings in about four seconds. With zero reviews you lose that comparison every time, even when you rank.
Feed the websiteReviews get published on your service and town pages, marked up so Google can show a star rating next to your result. Clean Method does exactly this.
Feed AI answersWhen an AI is asked for the best cleaner in Rockville, review volume and sentiment are part of how it picks names.
CompoundEvery review makes the next one easier to get and makes every job you have already done keep working for you.

How the review routine actually works

This is a routine built into how you close a job, not a piece of software.

  • The technician asks in person at completion, while the customer is happy
  • A text goes out within the hour with a one-tap link straight to the review box
  • One reminder at day three, then it stops
  • Every review gets a reply within 24 hours, naming the service and the town
  • Steady beats sudden. Five to ten a month reads as real. Forty in one week reads as bought, and Google filters it
  • For trauma and biohazard work, ask carefully or not at all. Many of those clients will not want to be publicly connected to the job. Never push

One part of this only you can do. Business to business reviews come from a phone call, not an automation. The first twenty need to come from you personally calling clients you have already served. We build the system, write the scripts and templates, and handle every reply. The calls are yours.

What is broken on the website

Every item was found by crawling your live site on 22 August. Nothing here is estimated.

Critical
Your homepage is 7.6 MB. It should be under 1 MB.
Six homepage images are over 900 KB each. The largest, Commerical-Buildings.png, is 1,297 KB, and the filename has a typo in it. They are saved as PNG, a format meant for logos rather than photographs. On a phone on mobile data that is the difference between the page appearing in about one second and about eight. Most people leave first. Your hosting is fine, the server responds in 0.28 seconds. It is only the images.
Critical
CDC, EPA and OSHA logos are on your homepage
All three are federal agencies that restrict use of their seals. Placing them beside your certifications implies an endorsement that does not exist. You are pursuing federal contracting work, which makes this worse than it would be for anyone else. Ten minutes to remove. We replace them with wording that is equally persuasive and is true: "Follows CDC and EPA disinfection guidance", "OSHA bloodborne pathogen compliant".
Critical
Google does not know where your business is
Your site carries no machine-readable business data. No phone number, no address, no service area, no coordinates in a format Google reads. Your street address, 121 Talbott St, appears nowhere on the website at all, despite being on your Chamber and HomeAdvisor listings. Cheapest fix in this document, largest effect.
Critical
Four pages. Eleven services. Three states.
Homepage, About, Contact and one shared Services page. Nothing on your site can rank for "biohazard cleanup Montgomery County" or "medical facility cleaning Bethesda" because no page is about those things. Your highest margin work is currently a bullet point.
High
Three of four pages have no main heading
Services, About and Contact have no H1. A common page-builder default that nobody changes. Your homepage has one, but it reads "Precision Cleaning, Every Time", which is a slogan. No service, no location, so it tells Google nothing.
High
Your homepage and services page compete with each other
"Professional Cleaning Services in DMV | Black Ops Cleaning" against "Professional Cleaning Services DMV | Black Ops Cleaning Co." Nearly identical, so Google has to guess which to show and neither builds authority.
High
Your page descriptions sell residential cleaning
"For homes and businesses" on the homepage, "residential, commercial and specialized" on the services page. Everything else about you, ISSA and GBAC certification, your imagery, your own positioning, is commercial, medical and government. 22 of 32 homepage images have no description text
Empty alt attributes. On a page that is mostly images that is a wasted signal and a page nobody using a screen reader can navigate. Worth noting given you are bidding for government work, where website accessibility gets looked at.
