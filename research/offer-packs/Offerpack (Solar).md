---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Real savings examples with bill comparisons** (the #1 hesitation for solar is "I don't know if the ROI is real" -- a homeowner's actual bill before and after is the highest-converting content in this niche, more powerful than any calculator)
2. **Local installer vs national brand positioning** (homeowners get pitched by national solar brands constantly; a local company that explicitly says "you'll talk to the same person from the quote to the install" converts the homeowner who is tired of being handed off)
3. **Permit, utility, and incentive handling statement** (the complexity of permits, net metering, and tax credits is the biggest reason homeowners delay; the installer who says "we handle all of this for you" removes the friction)
