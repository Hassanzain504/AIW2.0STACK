## Offer mechanics

**Risk reversal:** Free homepage design upfront. Paid on approval only.

**Price anchor (3-tier, they buy the middle):**

| Tier | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Homepage only, no SEO, 2 revision rounds | $800 |
| Growth (recommend this) | Full 5-page site, portfolio with project deep-dives, design process section, media/press section, mobile-optimized | $1,800 |

**Payment terms (Stage 1):** Free design upfront. On approval: 50% deposit, 50% on launch.

**The Starbucks guarantee:** "If I waste your time, I'll send you a $25 Starbucks gift card."

4. "the project came in on budget"

8. "worth every dollar -- and more"

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Project deep-dives with before, process, and after** (design clients are buying a relationship and a process, not just the finished product; a project page that shows the brief, the design development, and the final result converts the high-value client who wants to understand what working with you looks like)
2. **Named design philosophy or signature style statement** (designers who articulate a clear point of view attract clients who want exactly that; a homepage that says "I do X for clients who want Y" filters in ideal clients and filters out the wrong ones)
3. **Press, awards, and notable projects section** (design clients at the $50k+ level check credibility signals the same way a business client would -- publications, awards, or notable collaborations convert the sophisticated buyer who knows the market)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.
