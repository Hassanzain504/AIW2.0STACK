## Why landscaping

1. Less agency competition than roofing or HVAC. Most specialist agencies (Landscape Leadership, LawnCareWebDesign, FreshySites, MetricsLion) are either too expensive for small operators or too generic to convert homeowners. No dominant national platform owns this niche the way Scorpion owns HVAC or Roofing Webmasters owns roofing.

2. The deposit ghost is an unaddressed fear. The #1 negative theme across 468 reviews is contractors who take a deposit and disappear. No competitor website addresses this. A deposit protection statement built into every client site is an immediate, visible differentiator.

3. High-value install tickets ($12,000-$20,000 per backyard transformation) make the ROI story clean. One extra closed project per month covers the website investment in the first week.

---

## Key differentiator Hassan will build into every site

Homeowner-centric conversion: visual aspiration gallery (filterable before/after, labeled by project type and style), deposit protection statement, "bring my vision to life" copy language, and clear distinction between maintenance and design/install buyer journeys.

---

## Risks acknowledged

- Split buyer persona (maintenance vs design/install) requires two conversion flows in the template. Accepted.
- Highly visual niche; client must have quality job photos. Client onboarding checklist will include a photo audit.
- Seasonal revenue. Mitigated by positioning around design/install (spring planning season) and year-round maintenance upsell.

## 5 example businesses Hassan could approach this week

1. Any independent landscaping contractor in his target US city with fewer than 50 Google reviews and no clear before/after gallery on their site. Search "landscaping company [city]" on Google Maps and filter by rating 3.5-4.2 (strong enough to close clients, weak enough to need help).

2. Contractors running Facebook ads with no landing page - just sending ad traffic to a generic homepage with no project gallery. This is visible in the Facebook Ads Library by searching "landscaping" in any US metro.

3. Lawn care operators who have been in business 3+ years (indicates consistent revenue) but whose website still shows stock photography. This signals they have real clients and real jobs but have not invested in documentation.

4. Landscaping contractors active on Instagram or Houzz with strong visual content but no website that matches the quality of their social posts. Their content is already there; the site just needs to use it.

5. Referral from the painting contractor Hassan already worked with. Painting and landscaping contractors often share the same residential customer base and frequently refer to each other. Ask the painter who does their clients' landscaping.
