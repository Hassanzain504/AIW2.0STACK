Teardown for Module 2D

The bar is
lower than expected

Eight US sites checked page by page, looking specifically at how their town pages, service pages and service plus town pages are built. Two are worth copying. The rest, including three national franchises, publish town pages that are the same paragraph with the city name swapped.

The best complete example is not a cleaning company at all, and it operates in Rockville.

  • The service plus town page has no reviews. All the proof sits one level up on the town page and never reaches the page that actually gets the search.

  • No link back up to the town page. The mesh runs down but not up, which is a real internal linking hole in a site otherwise built for this.

02

The local hook

Roto-Rooter solves the hardest problem in a town page without needing a case study, and the technique transfers straight to cleaning.

A town page has to say something true that only applies to that town. Our template answers that with a real local job and a photograph, which is the strongest answer. But that gates every town page on the client supplying photographs, and photographs arrive slowly.

Roto-Rooter has a second answer running alongside it: explain why this town has this problem. Rockville has clay heavy soil, so pipes shift. It has mature street trees, so roots invade. Its older neighbourhoods still run cast iron and galvanised lines. None of that can be swapped to another city, and none of it needed a client photograph.

The cleaning equivalent

Roto-Rooter usesA cleaning page would use
Clay soil and pipe movement The local building stock. Rockville Town Center is newer glass and open plan; the Pike corridor is 1980s office; Twinbrook is older low rise. Each cleans differently.
Mature street trees and root intrusion The local industry mix. Shady Grove and the I-270 corridor are biotech and medical, which means infection control and after hours access, not general janitorial.
Mid-Atlantic storms and basement flooding The same storms, ending in sewage backup and water damage decontamination, which is a service Black Ops already sells.
Older cast iron and galvanised lines County and state inspection regimes, and what a failed infection control inspection costs a practice in this county.
Named local manager and address 121 Talbott St. The crew starts the day in Rockville rather than driving in from another county, which is a real and checkable advantage.

Change this in the town page schema

Add an optional localHook block: two or three sentences on why this town produces this kind of work, drawn from building stock, industry mix, weather or regulation. It is not a replacement for the required proof job, it is what carries the page in the weeks before a photograph from that town exists.

It also unblocks a real scheduling problem. Right now no town page can be built until the client supplies a photograph. With a local hook, the page can be written and reviewed while the photograph is still outstanding, and the build gate stays where it is.

The finding that matters commercially

Clean Method beats Black Ops on everything, and its location pages are state level. Spaulding Decon is a national category leader whose Maryland page lists one city. Vanguard has no local proof at all.

So the seventeen town pages and nineteen service plus town pages in the proposal are not competing against good work. They are competing against boilerplate with the city name swapped in. A town page carrying one real local job and a photograph is genuinely rare, and that is exactly the page the template refuses to build without.
