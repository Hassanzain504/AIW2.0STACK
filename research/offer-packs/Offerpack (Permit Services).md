1. "My contractor said it would take two weeks. It took four months."
2. "I didn't know permit expediting was even a thing until my project stalled."
3. "The city kept sending rejections and I didn't know what to fix."

5. "My GC recommended a permit runner and it saved us six weeks."
6. "I just needed someone who speaks the language of these officials."
7. "We couldn't start construction until the permit was approved. Every day of delay cost money."
8. "I searched online but couldn't find anyone local who did this."

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Turnaround time and jurisdiction coverage** (permit buyers are in a hurry and geographically specific, showing exactly which cities and counties you cover and your average approval time is the first trust hurdle)
2. **Process transparency** (contractors and homeowners are often confused by the permit process, a clear step-by-step on your site shows expertise and removes fear)
3. **Client type specificity** (a GC and a homeowner have different permit needs, a site that speaks to each separately builds instant relevance)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
