# SOP 10 - Client Intake
Stage 1. Niche: landscaping.
Generated by Module 2D at 2026-06-01T00:00:00Z.

## Purpose
Collect all inputs for the build. For landscaping clients, photos and service area are the critical blockers.

## Procedure
1. Collect: business name, short name, tagline, owner name and title, phone and email, service area cities, license number, insurance confirmation, Google Business Profile URL, social links, services offered, buyer journey type, years in business, projects completed, any awards.
2. Check photos. Minimum: 1 hero photo, 10 gallery photos, 1 owner headshot. Pause if missing.
3. Confirm deposit protection body copy or apply default from copy-deck.json.
4. Write intake-form.json.

## Pass criteria
- intake-form.json complete with no empty required fields
- Minimum photo set present in Assets/photos/
- Service area has at least 3 specific cities

## When this stage halts
- Client cannot provide any project photos
- No license number available

## Niche-specific notes
Service area list is a local SEO signal. Push for 8 to 12 specific suburbs over a vague metro name.
Buyer journey type (maintenance-only vs design/install vs both) affects copy emphasis. Record in intake-form.json.
