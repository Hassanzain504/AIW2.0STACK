What the work covers

Everything below is included in a standard remediation. Nothing here is an upsell added later.

Assessment and containment

The affected area is isolated before anything is touched, so contamination does not spread through the property.

Removal of affected material

Flooring, subfloor, drywall and furnishings that cannot be safely restored are removed and documented.

Decontamination

Hospital grade disinfection of all affected surfaces, following CDC and EPA disinfection guidance.

Odour remediation

Treated at the source rather than masked. Masking is why odour returns three weeks later.

Regulated waste disposal

Transported and disposed of through a licensed medical waste stream, with the manifest given to you.

Clearance documentation

Written confirmation the property is safe for occupancy, which is what insurers and estate solicitors ask for.

Questions people actually ask

Eight to twelve real questions, answered directly. This is the block AI answers pull from, so it is not optional.

Does homeowner insurance cover this?
Frequently, yes. Most Maryland homeowner policies treat unattended death remediation as a covered loss. We speak to the adjuster directly and bill them, so you are not paying up front and claiming back.
Will your vehicle be marked?
No. Our response vehicles carry no company signage. Neighbours see a plain van.
How quickly can you get here?
Within two hours across Montgomery County, at any hour. Rockville, Bethesda and Silver Spring are typically faster than that.
Do I have to be at the property?
No. We can work from a key handover or a lockbox and send you photographic documentation throughout.
Can the property be lived in afterwards?
Yes, and we issue written clearance confirming it. That document is what insurers, buyers and estate solicitors ask to see.
What if the odour comes back?
It should not. Odour that returns means the source was masked rather than removed. We treat at source, and we return at no charge if it recurs.
Do you handle the disposal paperwork?
Yes. Regulated waste goes through a licensed medical waste stream and the manifest is handed to you.
Are you police approved?
There is no such approval to hold. What matters is GBAC training, OSHA bloodborne pathogen compliance, and being licensed and insured in Maryland. We are all three, and any company claiming police endorsement is worth a second look.
