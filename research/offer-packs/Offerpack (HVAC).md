## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Emergency availability with response time commitment** (a broken AC in July or a dead furnace in January is not a consideration purchase -- the contractor who shows a response time wins before the others even pick up the phone)
2. **Upfront pricing statement** (HVAC has a reputation for surprise bills; one sentence saying the homeowner gets a written price before any work starts removes the #1 objection and no competitor puts it in their hero)
3. **Named technician profiles with certifications** (NATE-certified technicians named by first name with certification visible -- homeowners entering someone into their home need to know who is coming)

### Prospect 5: Any HVAC contractor with 20 to 80 Google reviews, running Google Ads, but sending traffic to a slow or generic homepage
**How to find them:** Search "AC repair [target US city]" on Google. Look at the sponsored results. Run those URLs through PageSpeed Insights (pagespeed.web.dev). If the mobile score is under 60, the contractor is paying per click and losing half those clicks before the page loads.

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
