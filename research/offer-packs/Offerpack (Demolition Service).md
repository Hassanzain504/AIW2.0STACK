## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Licensing, insurance, and permit-pulling capability** (demolition is high-stakes. homeowners and GCs who hire unlicensed crews get stuck with liability and failed inspections. being the contractor who names licensing and permit handling removes the top risk concern)
2. **Debris removal and haul-off included** (homeowners and contractors often don't know whether demo includes cleanup. stating that debris removal is part of the job removes a common hesitation that sends prospects to look elsewhere)
3. **Utility identification and safe disconnection process** (the biggest fear in demolition is hitting a live wire or gas line. a brief statement about how you identify and manage utilities before starting builds trust with both homeowners and GC partners)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
