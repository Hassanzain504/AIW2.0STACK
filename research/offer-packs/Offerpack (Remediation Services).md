## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **IICRC certification and state license visible in the hero** (remediation work affects health -- mold and asbestos especially; the homeowner calling is scared, not shopping; the company that shows certification credentials immediately converts the fear into a call)
2. **Insurance claim assistance statement** (most remediation work is insurance-covered; the company that explicitly says "we work directly with your insurance adjuster and handle the documentation" wins every post-disaster call)
3. **Before/after remediation photos with clearance test mention** (homeowners want to see the problem gone and the space safe -- an "after" photo paired with a mention of post-remediation clearance testing converts the skeptical homeowner who wonders if the problem is really fixed)
