## Offer mechanics

**Risk reversal:** Free homepage design upfront. Paid on approval only.

**Price anchor (3-tier, they buy the middle):**

| Tier | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Homepage only, no SEO, 2 revision rounds | $800 |
| Growth (recommend this) | Full 5-page site, basic local SEO, project gallery by service type (shower, railing, mirror), mobile-optimized | $1,800 |
| Pro | Full site + GBP optimization + 3-month SEO content plan + quote request automation | $3,500 |

**Payment terms (Stage 1):** Free design upfront. On approval: 50% deposit, 50% on launch. After first case study: 30% deposit, 70% on launch.

**The Starbucks guarantee:** "If I waste your time, I'll send you a $25 Starbucks gift card. At least let me show you where I see the missed opportunities."

## Customer language to echo in every DM

1. "I wanted frameless, not that cheap framed stuff from the hardware store."
2. "We needed someone who actually measures and cuts on site, not just installs pre-made panels."
3. "The shower glass completely changed how the bathroom looks."
4. "I searched for a while before I found someone who specializes in this."
5. "I needed to see examples before I trusted someone with my new bathroom remodel."
6. "The glass railing on the stairs makes the whole house feel more open."
7. "Our designer recommended them but I still looked up their website first."
8. "Response time mattered a lot. The first guy never called back."

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Service-specific photo gallery** (buyers shopping for shower glass, railings, and mirrors each have a different mental image of what they want, a gallery organized by category helps them self-identify)
2. **Specialty and licensing signals** (glass work is safety-critical, buyers want proof of insurance and professional credentials before letting someone into their home)
3. **Fast quote or measurement request form** (the buying decision often starts with a free measurement visit, make that step dead simple)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
