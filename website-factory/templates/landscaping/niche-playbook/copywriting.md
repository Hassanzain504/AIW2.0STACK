# Copywriting Guide - Landscaping Niche

## Voice

First-person plural throughout. "We build", "our crew", "we show up".
Never "I" unless it is the owner speaking directly in a bio.

## Homeowner language rules

- "Your yard", "your project", "your budget" - always your, never "the" or "our client's"
- "Your vision" is a recurring phrase. Use it in the hero and in the deposit protection section.
- Address the reader directly. "Before any work begins, you approve the plan."

## Specificity rules

- Specific nouns over generic nouns: "natural stone patio" not "outdoor feature"
- Named services, not categories: "weekly lawn mowing" not "maintenance services"
- Investment ranges in the gallery, not vague "contact for pricing"
- Specific timeframes: "2 to 5 days" not "a few days"

## Sentence structure

- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Declarative statements. "We show up on time." Not "We aim to arrive punctually."
- No em-dashes. Use a period or a comma.
- No buzzwords from the blocked list in vocabulary.json.

## CTA copy

Use the locked CTAs from copy-locks.json. Do not invent new primary CTAs.
Primary CTA is always "Get My Free Estimate".

## Section-specific guidance

### Hero H1
Must include the city name or service region. Must sound like a local business, not a national chain.
Good: "Your Vision. Our Work. [City] Landscaping Specialists."
Good: "A Yard You Are Proud to Come Home To."
Avoid: "Transform Your Outdoor Living Space" (buzzword, vague)

### Deposit protection section
Body copy must use "written contract" not "agreement" or "proposal".
Must mention scope, timeline, and payment schedule explicitly.
Must be written to a homeowner who has been burned before.

### Gallery captions
Format: [project type label] / [neighborhood or city area] / [investment range]
Example: "Natural stone patio / Oak Park / $9,000-$13,000"

### Social proof
Use first name and neighborhood. "Jane S. in Elmhurst" not full name.
Let the review text do the work. Do not editorialize.
