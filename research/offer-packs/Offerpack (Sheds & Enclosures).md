Hassan has 4 years of freelance web dev experience but no shed and enclosure-specific case studies and no existing client in this niche. The correct offer shape is:

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Completed build gallery organized by shed type and size** (homeowners are visual buyers and need to see a structure similar to what they want before they believe it is possible)
2. **Custom vs. kit comparison section** (the primary competitor is not another builder, it is the Home Depot kit, so a clear cost and quality comparison on the site converts fence-sitters)
3. **Permit handling or zoning guidance note** (many homeowners do not know what permits they need, and a contractor who addresses this upfront removes a major anxiety point)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
