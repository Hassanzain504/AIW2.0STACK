## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Energy savings calculator or before/after bill comparison** (homeowners won't call an insulation company until they believe the ROI -- a simple "average savings: $1,200/year" statement or a bill comparison converts the skeptic)
2. **Rebate and tax credit section** (federal and state insulation rebates can cover 30%+ of the cost -- the contractor who mentions this first on the homepage wins the call from homeowners who didn't know it existed)
3. **Specific product and R-value information** (homeowners Googling insulation are comparing R-values -- the site that explains what they're installing and why converts the research stage into a call)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.
