Justification five: the cost of waiting

Doing nothing is also a decision with a price attached.

Right now, every one of these is running against you

  • You are not on Google Maps. Every local search shows a competitor's listing and not yours. That is happening today, and every day, at no cost to them.
  • You rank for nothing. Zero search terms. Your competitors hold 877, 1,400 and 3,900.
  • You are cited by no AI engine. Zero across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode and Gemini. One competitor has 152 pages cited.
  • Your review count stays at zero. Reviews compound, which means the gap widens every month rather than staying still.
  • Federal agency logos are still on your homepage, while you bid for federal work.

On the audit's own conservative model, that is roughly one job a month going elsewhere. Waiting a quarter to decide is not a neutral choice. It is the price of this proposal, again, in work that went to somebody else.

What I need from you

Two of these decide whether the work reaches its full value. Neither can be bought.

  1. Account access in week one. Google Business Profile, Search Console, Analytics, hosting, DNS and the website admin.
  2. Job photographs, five per completed job, weekly. This is what replaces the stock photography permanently and feeds the town pages, the service pages and the Google profile. Nothing else fixes it.
  3. The review calls. I build the system, write the scripts and handle every reply. The first twenty reviews have to come from you personally calling clients you have already served. Business to business reviews come from a phone call, not an automation.
  4. The 24/7 decision, in week one. Every biohazard competitor in this market advertises it. If you will not answer at 3am, I will not build the emergency pages, because they would rank and convert at nothing.
  5. Licence and insurance numbers, and your SAM.gov details. Needed for the trust bar and for the government-facing content.
  6. Replies to drafts within three working days. The eight week timeline assumes this.
