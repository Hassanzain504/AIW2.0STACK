The constraint that shapes all four

Every competitor in the teardown carries Home, About and Contact on social proof. Molly Maid shows 1,079 reviews before you scroll. Bio-One puts the owners and a Google rating badge in the hero. Black Ops has zero reviews on every platform and no supplied job photographs.

Padding around that with adjectives is what a weak site does, and a reader can feel it. The alternative is to substitute things that are verifiable: a real street address with a map, a UEI a buyer can look up, a named owner with a face, a written specification, a licence number, a thirty day exit. Checkable beats flattering, and it is the only honest option until the review system fills the slots.

So every amber slot in this batch is a real gap with a date on it, not a placeholder to write around. Home ships with the review block built and empty rather than built and invented.

The cannibalisation rule for this batch

Two pairs can collide, and both collisions are avoidable.

Home against the service pages. Home does not target commercial cleaning Rockville MD. That belongs to /services/commercial-cleaning/ and it is the strongest commercial term on the site. Home targets the company, not the service.

Government Contracting against the government facility page. The service page answers can you clean my federal building. This page answers are you a company I can put on a contract. Different question, different reader, different search. The test: if a block would make sense on the service page, it does not belong here.

Block 04, certifications explained

What our certifications actually mean

Most cleaning sites list badges and explain none of them. A buyer comparing three contractors is looking at the same unexplained logos on all three. Explaining them is free, and it is the kind of depth AI answers pull from.

GBAC

The Global Biorisk Advisory Council, a division of ISSA. GBAC training covers how to respond to biological contamination properly, including the parts most people get wrong: contact time, clean to dirty sequencing, and containment. It is the reason we can do infection control in a clinical space and biohazard remediation with the same standard behind both.

What it means for you: the disinfection is done to a method, and the method is written down.

ISSA and CMI

ISSA is the worldwide cleaning industry association. The Cleaning Management Institute is its training arm, and CMI certification is training for individual technicians rather than a rating of the company.

What it means for you: the person in your building was trained to a published standard, not shown around by whoever left last month.

Bloodborne pathogen training

Required under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1030 for anyone who can reasonably be expected to encounter blood or other potentially infectious material at work. It comes with an exposure control plan, and ours is a document we can show you.

What it means for you: the crew is legally trained for the work, and your liability is not sitting on our shortcut.

SAM.gov registration

Registration in the federal contracting system, UEI PMPPFTB7CCX4, under NAICS 561720. You can look it up without asking us.

What it means for you: if you are a public body, we are already able to be paid by you.

Slot, blocking, confirm what is held

Do not publish a single one of these until it is confirmed. GBAC trained, GBAC STAR accredited, ISSA member and CMI certified are four different claims. Get the certificate, the number, the holder and the expiry for each.

CIMS and Green Seal. Still outstanding from batch three, and federal and institutional solicitations name CIMS specifically. If Black Ops does not hold it, this page should not imply that ISSA membership is the same thing.

Block 03, how to buy

How to actually contract with us

Most capability statements stop at the codes and leave the buyer to work out the mechanics. This block is the difference between a page that qualifies us and a page that starts a contract.

Small purchases

For work under the micro-purchase threshold, a government purchase card is usually the fastest route and needs no solicitation. We accept card payment.

Simplified acquisition

Below the simplified acquisition threshold, procurements are generally reserved for small business. We are registered, we quote on RFQs, and we can turn a straightforward janitorial quote around quickly.

Full solicitations

We respond to RFPs and IFBs where the scope fits. If it does not fit, we will tell you during the question period rather than submitting something we cannot perform.

Subcontracting to a prime

We take subcontract work, and for a prime the calculation is usually simple: a small, veteran owned, locally based subcontractor with in house biohazard capability and no subcontracting chain of its own. Ask us about capacity and you will get an answer the same day, not after the submission deadline.

Slot, blocking, the thresholds

No dollar figure appears in this block and none should be added without verification. The micro-purchase and simplified acquisition thresholds are set in the FAR and they are adjusted for inflation periodically.

Verify both against the current FAR before publishing, and then decide whether to print the numbers at all. Describing the mechanism without the figure ages well. A stale number on a page read by contracting officers does not, and it is the kind of error that costs credibility in one glance.

Slot, do we accept purchase cards

Confirm with Amilcar. If the answer is no, this is worth fixing, because the purchase card is the easiest way for a federal customer to try a contractor with almost no paperwork. A first card job is the cheapest past performance a small business can buy.

Block 04, past performance

Past performance

Three to six entries. Facility type, scope, square footage, contract length and value band, and a reference contact where the client agrees.

Slot, blocking, and the one that must not be dressed up

Nothing here is known. Answer it honestly and specifically.

If the federal record is empty and the commercial and county record is strong, say exactly that: new to federal prime work, with this commercial record behind it, and here is a reference who will take your call.

Contracting officers verify past performance. An overstatement here does not cost one contract, it ends the relationship permanently and it can put the registration at risk. There is no version of this page where a vague claim beats a short honest list.

Commercial past performance counts. A county or municipal contract counts for a great deal. Say which is which.
