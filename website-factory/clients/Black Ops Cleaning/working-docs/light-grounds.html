What I would pick, and why

C, warm off-white. Look at A beside C in the seam strip. A is brighter, and over 1,800 words that brightness is the thing that tires the eye. C gives that back without losing any crispness.

Warm rather than cool, so compare C against E. E reads like a lab. That suits the medical and disinfection pages and it works against the trauma and hoarding ones. The clinical signal is already carried by the words, the protocols and the mono codes. The background does not need to say it twice, and if it does, nothing on the site is warm.

D is out on evidence, not taste. Look at its card: it has almost disappeared into the ground. It would need a different card fill to survive.

One rule the samples make visible: the brass button works on every ground, but brass as text would not. That is why the link and the outlined button use the darker brass.
