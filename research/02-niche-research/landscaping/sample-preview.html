Common Questions

What Homeowners Ask

How much does a backyard transformation cost?
Most full backyard projects in the Dallas area run between $12,000 and $28,000 depending on size, materials, and whether you are adding hardscape, planting, irrigation, or lighting. We give you a fixed-price proposal before any work starts.
What if I need to change the design after we start?
Changes happen. Our contract includes a clear change order process. Any scope change is documented in writing with the updated cost before we proceed. No surprises on the final invoice.
How long does a typical installation take?
A standard patio and planting project takes 3 to 5 days. Full yard transformations typically run 7 to 14 days. We give you a day-by-day schedule in your contract.
Do you offer financing?
Yes. We partner with GreenSky to offer 12-month same-as-cash financing on projects over $5,000. Ask us for details when you get your estimate.
