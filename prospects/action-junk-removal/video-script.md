# Paul, Action Junk Removal Seattle: exact 2-minute video script

Har step mein do cheezein hain:
- **KARO:** screen par kya karna hai (Urdu mein)
- **BOLO:** exact alfaaz jo bolne hain (English mein)

Kul takreeban 290 alfaaz hain, jo aaram se bolne par 2 minute bante hain.

## Paul ke liye kya alag hai (Bryan se)

1. **Uski apni rates demo mein hain.** $250 (1/4), $375 (1/2), $500 (3/4), $625 (full truck), plus tax. Yeh uski website ke pricing page se li gayi hain. Jab woh apne numbers dekhega to foran samjhega ke yeh uske liye bana hai.
2. **Uski apni website ki line use ki hai.** Uske pricing page par likha hai: *"On-site Estimates Required for Most Junk Hauls."* Video ka problem wala hissa isi line par bana hai.
3. **Seattle se Tacoma ki drive.** Uska office West Seattle mein hai aur woh Tacoma tak kaam karta hai. I-5 ki traffic mein ek estimate ka chakkar aasaani se 1 se 2 ghante le leta hai.
4. **Job types uske kaam ke hisaab se:** Junk removal, Cleanout, aur Debris.

---

## Recording se pehle (5 minute ki tayyari)

1. Computer par ek folder mein **junk pile ki 3 photos** rakhein. Google Images se "garage junk pile" ya "apartment cleanout junk" download kar lein. Screenshots use na karein.
2. `prospects/action-junk-removal/index.html` ko **Chrome** mein kholein.
3. Browser zoom **90%** kar dein (Ctrl aur minus) taake form aur phone dono ek saath screen par aayein.
4. Top par **"Restart demo"** dabayein taake sab khaali ho.
5. Baaki tabs aur notifications band karein. Phone silent par rakhein.
6. **Loom** kholein: "Screen + Camera" chunein, sirf yeh Chrome tab select karein, aur camera ka gol bubble **neeche left corner** mein rakhein.
7. Script 2 dafa zor se parhein. Phir record karein.

## Recording ke baad

1. Video ek dafa poori dekhein. 2:15 se lambi ho to dobara record karein.
2. Loom par title rakhein: **"Paul, your quote page for Action Junk Removal"**
3. Loom ka thumbnail woh frame rakhein jisme aapka chehra aur "Action Junk Removal" dono nazar aa rahe hon.
4. Link copy karke yeh email bhejein. Audit ki Word file bhi attach karein (`prospects/action-junk-removal/website-audit.docx`):

> **Subject:** Re: your 2-minute video
>
> Hi Paul,
>
> Here it is, about 2 minutes: [Loom link]
>
> I used your actual rates from your pricing page, so it should look familiar.
>
> While I was on your site I also noticed a few things that are likely costing you calls. I put them in a short audit, attached. The one I'd fix first: your quote form says you reply "inside of 12 hours Monday to Friday," but you haul 7 days a week.
>
> Quick question while you watch: how many estimates do you drive out to in a normal week?
>
> Hassan
