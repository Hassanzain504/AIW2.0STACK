## Stage of business

**Stage: No portfolio**

Hassan has 4 years of freelance web dev experience but no painting-specific case studies and no existing client in this niche. The correct offer shape is:

"I'll design your homepage upfront, for free. If you love it, you pay. If not, you walk. No invoice, no awkward follow-up."

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Before/after photo gallery** (homeowners are buying a visual result; the gallery is the primary conversion mechanism -- if a painter has no before/after photos on their site, they are invisible to the buyer who is imagining their own rooms)
2. **Prep work and cleanup statement** (the #1 fear is painters who rush prep, leave drips, and don't protect surfaces -- explicitly addressing this on the homepage converts better than any headline)
3. **Written scope before any work starts** (scope creep and hidden charges are the #1 complaint in negative painting reviews -- a "written quote with a full scope before we touch anything" statement removes this objection immediately)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
