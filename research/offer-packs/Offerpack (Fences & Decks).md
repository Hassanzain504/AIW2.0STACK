## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Project gallery with material type and cost ranges** (fence and deck buyers are in the planning stage -- a gallery showing cedar vs composite vs vinyl with investment ranges converts the undecided homeowner into a caller)
2. **Permit handling statement** (many homeowners don't know a permit is required for a deck or fence; the contractor who explicitly says "we handle all permits" removes a hidden anxiety and differentiates from competitors who don't mention it)
3. **Deposit protection and written scope** (fencing and decking jobs are often quoted and then not started for weeks -- the homeowner's #1 fear is paying a deposit to a contractor who disappears or changes the scope mid-job)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
