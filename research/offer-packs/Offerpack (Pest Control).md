## Stage of business

**Stage: No portfolio**

Hassan has 4 years of freelance web dev experience but no pest control-specific case studies and no existing client in this niche. The correct offer shape is:

"I'll design your homepage upfront, for free. If you love it, you pay. If not, you walk. No invoice, no awkward follow-up."

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Treatment process explained in plain language** (homeowners with kids and pets are terrified of chemicals -- the company that explains what they use, how it works, and how safe it is converts before the others even get a chance to quote)
2. **Same-day or next-day availability statement** (pest calls are triggered by urgency and embarrassment; the company who can come today wins the booking)
3. **Re-treatment guarantee** (the #1 fear is paying for a treatment and having the pests return -- a plain guarantee removes this and no competitor puts it in their hero)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
