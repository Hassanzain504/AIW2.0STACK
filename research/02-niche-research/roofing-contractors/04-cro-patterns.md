# 04 - CRO Patterns: Roofing Contractors

Niche: roofing-contractors

## Recommended Section Order (top to bottom)

1. **Sticky header** — phone number (tap-to-call on mobile), primary CTA button ("Free Inspection"), and logo. Always visible.
2. **Hero section** — headline targeting the primary fear or intent, sub-headline naming the city, CTA above the fold.
3. **Social proof bar** — Google review star rating + count, BBB badge, any manufacturer certification. Immediate trust after hero.
4. **Services strip** — 4-6 service tiles (Roof Replacement, Roof Repair, Storm Damage, Emergency Repair, Insurance Claims, Commercial). Scannable.
5. **Trust stack** — Years in business, number of roofs completed, warranty details, license number. Specifics outperform generic claims.
6. **Photo gallery** — Real before/after job photos. Minimum 12. Local markets if possible.
7. **How it works** — 3-step process: Inspection → Quote → Installation. Removes uncertainty about what happens next.
8. **Review section** — Minimum 6 embedded Google or Facebook reviews, full-text, with homeowner name and city.
9. **Service areas** — Named cities/neighborhoods. Signals local relevance to both homeowners and Google.
10. **Insurance claim section** — Dedicated block explaining claim navigation assistance. High-value for storm markets.
11. **FAQ** — 5-8 questions that match "People Also Ask" from SERP data: cost, timeline, warranty, insurance process.
12. **Final CTA section** — Repeat primary CTA with a friction-reducing line ("No obligation. We'll come to you.").
13. **Footer** — License number, address, service area list, phone, social links.

---

## Hero Composition

**Must-haves above the fold:**
- Headline that names a specific fear or outcome (not generic "quality roofing services")
- City or service area named in sub-headline
- Phone number visible without scrolling
- CTA button: "Get Free Inspection" or "Get a Quote"
- Background: real job-site photo or a roof in process, not stock imagery of smiling homeowners

**Avoid above the fold:**
- Sliders or carousels (slow, distracting, reduce conversion)
- Long paragraphs of text
- Navigation-heavy layouts that bury the CTA
- Generic stock photography (roofing is a credibility-visual trade)

## Trust Stack (priority order)

1. **Google review count and rating** — visible in the hero or immediately below. Format: "4.9 stars — 320 Reviews." Link to Google profile.
2. **Years in business** — "Serving [City] Since [Year]." Specific year outperforms "X years experience."
3. **License number** — displayed in footer and optionally in hero. Homeowners checking contractors on state licensing boards will trust contractors who display it proactively.
4. **Manufacturer certifications** — GAF Master Elite, Owens Corning Preferred Contractor, CertainTeed SELECT ShingleMaster. These signal training and warranty eligibility.
5. **BBB Accreditation badge** — BBB.org ranks for "roofing contractors near me" (confirmed in SERP data). BBB badge on-site reinforces the trust already associated with that domain.
6. **Insurance and bonding statement** — "Fully licensed, bonded, and insured." Homeowners fear liability for crew injuries; this reduces that fear.
7. **Warranty statement** — "X-year workmanship warranty" displayed prominently, not buried in fine print.

## Form Patterns

**Best-performing form type for roofing:** Short lead-capture form, not a full quote calculator.

**Optimal fields:**
- Name
- Phone number (primary; most roofing leads close by phone)
- Address or zip code (to confirm service area)
- Optional: "What do you need?" dropdown (Inspection / Repair / Replacement / Storm Damage / Insurance Help)

**Form placement:**
- One form in the hero or immediately below (above the fold on desktop)
- One form in the final CTA section
- Avoid forms that require more than 4-5 fields before the first contact

**Form trigger text:** "Get Your Free Inspection — No Obligation" outperforms "Contact Us" or "Get a Quote" based on low-friction framing.

---

## Sticky and Always-Visible Elements

1. **Sticky header with phone number** — on mobile, a tap-to-call button should always be in the top-right. This is the highest-converting element on roofing sites.
2. **Mobile sticky bottom bar** — "Call Now" | "Get Inspection" — two-button bar at bottom of mobile screen. Standard in high-converting home-services sites.
3. **Chat widget** — secondary; useful for after-hours leads. Position bottom-right. Do not auto-trigger; it reads as aggressive.

---

## Mobile-Specific Considerations

Over 70% of "roofer near me" searches happen on mobile. [inferred from national home-services search data]

- Phone number in the hero must be a tappable link (tel: protocol)
- Form fields must be large enough to tap without zooming
- Gallery should be swipeable, not click-heavy
- Page speed is a ranking and conversion factor: avoid heavy hero videos on mobile
- Sticky bottom CTA bar is essential; desktop CTA patterns do not translate to mobile

---

## Urgency Mechanisms

Roofing has natural urgency triggers that most sites fail to activate:

1. **Seasonal urgency:** "Book before the fall rush — inspection slots filling up" (August-October in northern markets)
2. **Storm urgency:** Dynamic banner triggered manually after a local storm event: "Storm in [City] on [Date]? Get a free inspection this week."
3. **Wait-time signal:** "We currently have X-week lead times — schedule your free inspection now to hold your slot."
4. **Price stability notice:** "Material prices change quarterly — lock in your quote this week."
