---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Before/after install photos with damage context** (homeowners need to see the functional outcome -- overflowing gutters causing damage vs. clean new gutters properly pitched -- not just the product itself)
2. **Gutter guard comparison section** (the upsell homeowners always ask about -- a plain comparison of guard types removes the "do I need this?" question and converts fence-sitters into higher-ticket jobs)
3. **Warranty and material options section** (seamless vs sectional, aluminum vs copper -- the homeowner who doesn't know the difference defaults to price; the site that explains the options gets the call from the buyer who wants quality)
