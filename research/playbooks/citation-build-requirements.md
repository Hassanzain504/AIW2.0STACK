# Citation Build: What You Need Before You Start

Citations are mechanical work, but the prep decides whether they help or hurt. Forty listings
with slightly different details are worse than no listings, because inconsistent data actively
confuses Google about which business is which.

Collect everything below before creating a single listing.

---

## 1. The master NAP sheet

This is the single most important artefact in the whole job. One sheet, locked, and every
listing copies from it character for character. Never retype from memory.

| Field | Rule | Black Ops example |
|---|---|---|
| Legal business name | Exactly as registered. No keywords added. | Black Ops Cleaning Company LLC |
| Trading name | Only if genuinely different and used publicly | Black Ops Cleaning Co. |
| Street address | Exact format, decide "St" vs "Street" once and never vary | 121 Talbott St |
| Suite / unit | Include or omit consistently | (confirm with client) |
| City, State, ZIP | Full ZIP, and ZIP+4 if you use it anywhere, use it everywhere | Rockville, MD 20852 |
| Primary phone | One number, one format, everywhere | (240) 221-1914 |
| Website URL | Decide www vs non-www, http vs https, trailing slash. Match what the site actually resolves to. | https://blackopscleaningco.com |
| Public email | A domain email, not Gmail. Many directories reject free email. | info@blackopscleaningco.com |

Two decisions to make once, up front:

- **Format.** "St" or "Street", "(240) 221-1914" or "240-221-1914". Pick one of each and never
  deviate. Google is tolerant of format differences but not of genuine mismatches, and
  consistency costs nothing.
- **Service area business or storefront.** If customers do not visit the premises, the address
  is hidden on Google but still submitted to most directories. Decide this before you start,
  because changing it later means editing every listing.

## 2. Business details every directory asks for

Write these once, store them, paste them everywhere.

- **Short description**, roughly 150 characters
- **Long description**, 500 to 750 characters
- **Categories**, primary plus 3 to 5 secondary. Different platforms use different taxonomies,
  so write down your intended category and map it per platform.
- **Full service list**, with a one-line description for each
- **Opening hours**, including holiday hours and whether emergency service is 24/7
- **Year established**
- **Number of employees**, or a band
- **Service area**, the full list of towns
- **Payment methods accepted**
- **Licence number**
- **Insurance and bonding details**
- **Certifications**, with membership numbers where they exist (ISSA, GBAC)
- **Social profile URLs**, all of them
- **Tax ID / EIN**, needed by BBB and some others

## 3. Images

- **Logo**, square, at least 720 x 720, transparent PNG and a white-background version
- **Cover image**, wide, roughly 1024 x 576
- **5 to 10 photographs**, real ones, compressed, under 5 MB each
- Descriptive filenames, not `IMG_4471.jpg`

## 4. Access you need from the client

This is where jobs stall. Get all of it in week one.

- **A business email inbox you can reach.** Verification links land here. Either shared access,
  or an alias that forwards to you.
- **Someone who will answer the phone for verification calls.** Many directories verify with an
  automated call or an SMS to the listed number. If nobody answers, the listing does not go
  live.
- **A password manager or a shared credentials sheet.** You will create 30 to 40 accounts.
  Losing track of them means the client cannot manage their own listings later, which is a bad
  handover.
- **Existing logins** for anything already claimed, so you edit rather than duplicate.

## 5. Platform-specific extras

| Platform | Extra requirement |
|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Video verification is common now. Someone must film the premises, signage, equipment and tools on request. |
| Yelp | Phone verification. Free to claim; ignore the sales calls that follow. |
| Bing Places | A Microsoft account. Can import from Google, which saves time. |
| Apple Business Connect | An Apple ID. |
| BBB | Application, business documents, and an annual fee. Not free. |
| LinkedIn company page | Can only be created from a personal LinkedIn profile with a verified company email. |
| Chamber of Commerce | Paid membership. Rockville Chamber already exists but the listing is broken; Montgomery County Chamber is separate and would be a new membership. |
| Veteran directories | Some ask for proof of veteran status. Have the DD-214 or equivalent ready. |
| ISSA and GBAC directories | Membership or certification number. |
| Data aggregators (Data Axle, Localeze) | Some free tiers, some paid. Worth doing because they feed hundreds of smaller sites automatically. |
| Nextdoor | Requires a Nextdoor account tied to a real local address. |

## 6. The tracking sheet

Build this before you build listings. One row per platform:

```
Platform | Listing URL | Username | Password location | Date submitted |
Verification method | Status | Date live | Notes
```

Without it you cannot tell what is pending, what failed verification, or what needs re-checking
in six months. It is also part of the handover: the client should end up owning a complete map
of their own online presence.

---

## 7. Traps worth knowing

- **Do not use a leased-listing service without understanding it.** Yext-style platforms push
  your data to a network, but the listings are rented. Stop paying and they revert or vanish.
  Manually built listings are permanent. For a small business, build manually.
- **Do not submit all 40 in one day.** Spread it over two to three weeks. It looks more natural
  and it lets you catch a data error before it is replicated 40 times.
- **Fix the website first.** Black Ops has no street address on its own site. The website is the
  reference source everything else should match, so publishing the address there comes before
  any citation work.
- **Clean before you build.** Search for existing and duplicate listings first. Removing a bad
  duplicate is worth more than adding a new listing.
- **Never keyword-stuff the business name.** "Black Ops Cleaning Rockville Biohazard" will get
  a Google listing suspended and will make every other citation inconsistent.
- **Expect rejections.** Some directories reject free email addresses, some reject service area
  businesses, some are simply broken. Budget for perhaps 10 percent not completing.

---

## 8. Realistic time

| Stage | Time |
|---|---|
| Gathering everything above from the client | 1 to 2 hours, mostly waiting |
| Building the master sheet and tracker | 1 hour |
| Auditing existing and duplicate listings | 1 to 2 hours |
| Building 30 to 40 listings | 8 to 10 hours, spread over 2 to 3 weeks |
| Chasing verifications | 1 to 2 hours, spread out |

Roughly 12 to 15 hours of actual work, but it runs across two to three weeks because
verification is not instant and is not in your control.
