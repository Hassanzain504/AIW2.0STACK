"I build websites for home accessibility contractors that turn worried family members into booked consultations."

## Stage of business

**Stage: No portfolio**

The correct offer shape is: "I'll design your homepage upfront, for free. If you love it, you pay. If not, you walk. No invoice, no awkward follow-up."

## Offer mechanics

**Risk reversal:** Free homepage design upfront. Paid on approval only.

**Price anchor (3-tier, they buy the middle):**

| Tier | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Homepage only, no SEO, 2 revision rounds | $800 |
| Growth (recommend this) | Full 5-page site, basic local SEO, modification service menu with photos, mobile-optimized | $1,800 |
| Pro | Full site + GBP optimization + 3-month SEO content plan + automation setup (free home assessment form + auto-reply with checklist) | $3,500 |

**Payment terms (Stage 1):** Free design upfront. On approval: 50% deposit, 50% on launch.

**The Starbucks guarantee:** "If I waste your time, I'll send you a $25 Starbucks gift card."

---

## Customer language to echo in every DM

1. "My mom keeps falling and I don't know what to do."
2. "Is our bathroom safe for someone with a walker?"
3. "How much does a grab bar installation actually cost?"
4. "We need a ramp but I don't know where to start."
5. "Will Medicare or insurance cover any of this?"
6. "Can they work around my dad's schedule?"
7. "I need someone trustworthy -- my parent can't be left with just anyone."
8. "Does it look clinical or does it blend in with the home?"

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Before and after project photos** (families need to see that modifications look tasteful and professional, not institutional, before they book)
2. **Insurance and Medicare navigation experience** (a contractor who mentions funding options immediately lowers the perceived cost barrier for the family)
3. **Certifications and senior care affiliations** (CAPS certification or OT referral relationships signal that this contractor understands the medical context, not just the carpentry)
