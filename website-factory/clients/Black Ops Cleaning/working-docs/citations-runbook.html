02

The filing order

Eight phases. Each one exists because doing it later would mean redoing earlier work.

PHASE 0Lock the reference sheet Half a day

Write every field once into a single document. Name, address, phone, URL, email, hours, categories, three descriptions, service list, service area, year founded, logo paths, NAICS, licence, EIN, UEI, ISSA and GBAC numbers.

Nothing gets filed until this exists. Every later phase copies from it and never retypes from memory. This is the entire difference between citation work that compounds and citation work that confuses Google.

PHASE 1Fix the website first Inside engagement one

The address currently appears nowhere on the site, and two map links resolve to the city of Rockville and the state of Virginia rather than to the business.

Citations are votes that a business exists at an address. Pointing thirty of them at a site that does not state that address is filing them at half strength. Add the address, the business data Google can read, and correct the map links before phase 2.

PHASE 2Google Business Profile Day one, then 1 to 3 weeks

Submit on day one regardless of what else is unfinished, because verification is the longest lead item in the whole engagement and it is outside your control.

Before starting the video verification, have ready: exterior signage or the premises, the address visible, equipment and branded material, and something showing control of the location such as a utility bill or lease. A failed video verification costs days.

Once verified, build it out properly: category, service area, all eleven services with descriptions, the products section which almost nobody in this market uses, attributes including veteran owned, twenty photographs, and questions seeded and answered before a competitor answers them.

PHASE 3Repairs, before any new filing 2 to 3 days

Merge the two Facebook pages. Confirm first which one keeps the history, because a merge cannot be undone. Get the Chamber listing restored, it is paid for already. Claim the Pegbo listing that still says is this your business. Correct Angi and HomeAdvisor to the locked details.

Doing this after new filings means auditing the new ones again for consistency against details you were still fixing.

PHASE 4Tier one core 1 to 2 days plus verification waits

Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, LinkedIn company page. File Bing after the Google profile verifies, because it imports straight from Google and takes minutes instead of a full manual entry.

Never solicit Yelp reviews. Yelp filters solicited reviews aggressively and it can affect the listing itself.

PHASE 5Aggregators Half a day, weeks to propagate

Data Axle and Localeze. These feed hundreds of smaller directories automatically, so they are worth more than the individual sites they populate. File them early because propagation is slow, and file them accurately because a wrong record propagates just as efficiently as a right one.

PHASE 6The credential gated ones 2 days, plus SBA separately

This is the phase that produces most of the value. Veteran directories, ISSA, GBAC, Restoration Industry Association, BBB, Montgomery County Chamber.

Check the ISSA and GBAC directories first. The client is already a member of both, so the listing may exist and simply be unpublished. That is a free win in ten minutes.

Start SBA VetCert now but do not wait on it. Certification takes weeks to months and the directory listing follows it. Treat it as a later win, not a phase 6 deliverable.

PHASE 7Local and general volume 1 day

REDI, ExploreRockville, Visit Montgomery, Nextdoor, Thumbtack, Manta, n49, Yahoo Local, then the standard directory set and the cleaning industry directories.

The local ones are worth more than the general ones. REDI and ExploreRockville are real local links from bodies Google already trusts. Foursquare and Hotfrog are consistency filler.

PHASE 8Verify and record Half a day, then monthly

A listing is not complete when it is submitted. It is complete when it is live, correct, and recorded. Walk the whole set, confirm each one renders publicly with the exact locked details, and log it.

Then search the business name plus Rockville and see what comes back. Anything you did not file, and any duplicate you did not create, is now on your list too.

04

How this goes wrong

Six failure modes, all of them common, all of them avoidable.

The details drift

Someone types Suite 200 on one listing and Ste 200 on the next, or uses a different tracking number on three directories. Inconsistent details actively confuse Google and can do more harm than filing nothing at all. The reference sheet exists to prevent exactly this, and it only works if nobody ever types from memory.

  • Free mail addresses get rejected. Several directories will not verify a Gmail address for a business. Get the domain email before phase 4, not during it.
  • You create a duplicate. Always search the directory for an existing listing before creating one. Unclaimed listings are common, and claiming beats creating every time. The client already has an unclaimed Pegbo listing as proof.
  • Hours change after filing. If the 24 hour decision arrives late, thirty listings have to be edited by hand. This is why it is a blocker and not a detail.
  • The verification postcard goes nowhere. Confirm the address receives post reliably before choosing postcard verification. Prefer video where it is offered.
  • Yelp reviews get solicited. Yelp filters solicited reviews and it can affect the listing. Open the channel, then leave it alone.
  • Nobody records the logins. A perfectly built set of listings that the client cannot access is not a deliverable. The tracking sheet is the deliverable.

If time runs short, cut phase 7, never phase 6

The veteran and industry directories are relevant, hard for competitors to copy, and point at the exact buyers the client wants. Twenty general directories do not come close to matching them. Volume is the part that is safe to drop.
