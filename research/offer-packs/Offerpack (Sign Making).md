## Offer mechanics

**Risk reversal:** Free homepage design upfront. Paid on approval only.

**Price anchor (3-tier, they buy the middle):**

| Tier | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Homepage only, no SEO, 2 revision rounds | $800 |
| Growth (recommend this) | Full 5-page site, basic local SEO, sign gallery by type, quote request form, turnaround info, mobile-optimized | $1,800 |
| Pro | Full site + GBP optimization + 3-month SEO content plan + automated quote intake with sign type selector | $3,500 |

**Payment terms (Stage 1):** Free design upfront. On approval: 50% deposit, 50% on launch. After first case study: 30% deposit, 70% on launch.

**The Starbucks guarantee:** "If I waste your time, I'll send you a $25 Starbucks gift card. At least let me show you where I see the missed opportunities."

## Customer language to echo in every DM

1. "I needed a monument sign that matched our building's exterior. Not just a generic box."
2. "The online sign companies were fast but the quality was terrible."
3. "I wanted someone who could design it with me, not just print what I sent."
4. "Channel letters were a lot more than I expected but they were worth it."
5. "My contractor needed the sign permit handled. Most shops don't do that."
6. "I looked at a lot of portfolios before I found a shop with the style I was going for."
7. "We needed it installed, not just delivered. That made the difference."
8. "Turnaround mattered. We had a grand opening and couldn't miss it."

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Portfolio organized by sign type** (buyers looking for channel letters, monument signs, vehicle wraps, and window graphics each have a specific visual goal, a gallery by category helps them self-identify and builds confidence fast)
2. **Design-to-install capability** (many buyers don't want to manage multiple vendors, a shop that handles concept, fabrication, permitting, and installation wins on convenience)
3. **Local and commercial project experience** (business owners want to see that you have worked with similar businesses in their market, local references convert better than national samples)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
