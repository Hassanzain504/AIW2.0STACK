# Bryan, Real Deal Property Services: exact 2-minute video script (v2)

Har step mein do cheezein hain:
- **KARO:** screen par kya karna hai (Urdu mein)
- **BOLO:** exact alfaaz jo bolne hain (English mein)

Kul takreeban 280 alfaaz hain, jo aaram se bolne par 2 minute bante hain.

## Pichhle version se kya behtar kiya

1. **Pehli line mein Bryan ki apni baat.** Video unhi alfaaz se shuru hoti hai jo aapki pehli email mein the: "quote requests land with you personally." Is se Bryan ko foran lagega ke video sirf usi ke liye bani hai, template nahi.
2. **Pehle 10 second mein result.** Shuru mein hi bata diya ke video ke end mein kya dikhega, taake Bryan aakhir tak dekhe.
3. **Ridgecrest ka asli masla.** Ridgecrest phaila hua shehar hai. Inyokern ya shehar ke kinare tak ja kar wapas aana aadha ghanta kha jaata hai. Masla usi ki zameen ki misaal se samjhaya hai.
4. **Waqt ka hisaab, magar uski taraf se koi number nahi ghara.** "Agar hafte mein 5 quotes dekhne jaate ho to..." Is tarah woh khud apna hisaab lagata hai.
5. **"Log photos nahi bhejenge" wala shak pehle hi jawab mein.**
6. **Aakhir mein aasaan sawal.** "Yes" ya ek number reply karna aasaan hai. Lamba jawab likhna mushkil.

---

## Recording se pehle (5 minute ki tayyari)

1. Computer par ek folder mein **junk pile ki 3 photos** rakhein. Google Images se "garage junk pile" ya "backyard debris" download kar lein. Screenshots use na karein.
2. `prospects/real-deal-property-services/index.html` ko **Chrome** mein kholein.
3. Browser zoom **90%** kar dein (Ctrl aur minus) taake form aur phone dono ek saath screen par aayein.
4. Page **refresh** karein taake form khaali ho.
5. Baaki saare tabs aur notifications band kar dein. Phone silent par rakhein.
6. **Loom** kholein: "Screen + Camera" chunein, sirf yeh Chrome tab select karein, aur camera ka gol bubble **neeche left corner** mein rakhein.
7. Script 2 dafa zor se parhein. Phir record karein.

## STEP 1: Hook (0:00 se 0:12)

**KARO:** Page top par hai, "Real Deal Property Services" nazar aa raha hai. Kuch click na karein. Camera mein dekh kar muskurayein.

**BOLO:**
> "Hey Bryan, Hassan here. You said quote requests land with you personally, so I built this around you. In two minutes I'll show you how a customer gets you a quote without you leaving the house."

---

**BOLO:**

## STEP 10: Close (1:48 se 2:02)

**KARO:** Mouse chhod dein. Camera mein dekhein.

**BOLO:**
> "This is a demo with your name on it. The real one is a link you put on Facebook, your Google listing, or a sticker on the truck. If you want it live on your number, just reply 'yes' and I'll set it up this week."

**KARO:** 2 second muskura kar rukein, phir Loom ka **Stop** dabayein.

## Recording ke baad

1. Video ek dafa poori dekhein. 2:15 se lambi ho to dobara record karein.
2. Loom par title rakhein: **"Bryan, your quote page for Real Deal"**
3. Loom ka **thumbnail** woh frame rakhein jisme aapka chehra aur "Real Deal Property Services" dono nazar aa rahe hon. Email mein yahi pehle dikhta hai.
4. Link copy karke yeh email bhejein:

> **Subject:** Re: requests that land with you
>
> Hi Bryan,
>
> Here it is, about 2 minutes: [Loom link]
>
> Quick question while you watch: in a normal week, how many quotes do you drive out to look at?
>
> Hassan

---

## Agar Bryan reply kare

| Bryan ka jawab | Aap kya likhein |
|---|---|
| **"Yes"** | "Great. I need 10 minutes on a call to get your business details for the texting setup. Does tomorrow or Thursday work better?" |
| **Ek number, jaise "5 or 6"** | "That's a few hours a week on the road just quoting. Want me to set it up so most of those come in as photos instead? Takes about 10 minutes on a call." |
| **"How much?"** | Price email mein na batayein. "Depends on a couple of things on your end. Can I ask you 3 quick questions on a 10-minute call and give you an exact number?" |
| **"Customers won't send photos"** | "Fair. They can still just call you. This only catches the ones who'd rather not, like people messaging at 10pm. Want to try it for a few weeks and see?" |

---

## Agar galti ho jaaye

- **Koi lafz bhool gaye:** rukein nahi, apne alfaaz mein bol dein. Natural lagna exact alfaaz se zyada zaroori hai.
- **Galat button dab gaya:** page refresh karein aur us step se dobara shuru karein. Loom mein shuru ka hissa baad mein kaat sakte hain.
- **Photos upload nahi huin:** dekhein ke files JPG ya PNG hain.
