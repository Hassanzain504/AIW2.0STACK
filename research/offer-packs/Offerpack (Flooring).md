## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Before/after gallery with material type and room labels** (flooring is a visual purchase -- homeowners are imagining their specific room with specific materials; a gallery labeled by hardwood/LVP/tile and by room type converts the browser into a caller)
2. **Material and cost-per-sqft context** (flooring has massive price variation from carpet to marble -- a simple cost guide section on the homepage removes the "I don't know if I can afford this" hesitation that stops people from calling)
3. **Subfloor and prep statement** (hidden subfloor problems that drive up the final cost are the #1 negative review trigger in flooring; an upfront "we assess and disclose all subfloor conditions before quoting" statement removes the fear of surprises)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
