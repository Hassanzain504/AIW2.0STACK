## Stage of business

Hassan has 4 years of freelance web dev experience but no tree service-specific case studies and no existing client in this niche. The correct offer shape is:

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Liability insurance and ISA certification visible in the hero** (tree removal near a house or power lines is high-risk work; the #1 homeowner fear is a tree falling on the roof with no insurance to cover it -- showing the insurance and ISA certification converts better than any headline)
2. **Before/after job photos with debris cleanup pairs** (homeowners want to see the tree gone and the yard clean -- the "after cleanup" photo is the conversion image, not the crew in the tree)
3. **Same-day or emergency storm response statement** (a tree on a fence or blocking the driveway after a storm is an emergency; the company that shows emergency availability wins that call)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
