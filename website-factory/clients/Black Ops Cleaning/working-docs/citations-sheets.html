The locked reference sheet

Phase 0 of the runbook. Nothing gets filed until every field with a blocker tag is answered. Each field has a copy button, because the whole point is that the same characters land on all forty listings.

Three fields decide everything else

The legal name, the phone number and the hours go on every listing. Change one after filing and forty listings have to be edited by hand. Get the client to confirm these three in writing before phase 2 starts.

At handover this sheet goes to the client

Every account is registered in their name and every login is recorded here. A perfectly built set of listings the client cannot access is not a deliverable. This is what makes the no lock-in promise in the proposal real.

Field values are saved into this page itself, so they follow the link on any device and survive a browser reset. Anyone you share the link with can read them, so keep passwords out of it. Record account names here and store passwords in a password manager.

Wait times and verification methods are typical rather than quoted. Confirm each at filing time.
