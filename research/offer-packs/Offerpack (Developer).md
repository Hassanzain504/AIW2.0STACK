OFFER PACK, Residential and Commercial Property Developer Websites

---

## Core positioning sentence

"I build websites for property developers that give homebuyers the confidence to book a showing, request a floor plan, or join a waitlist before the first slab is poured."

## One-line public offer

"I design websites for residential and commercial developers that turn site visitors into qualified buyer leads."

(For IG bio variant: "I build property developer websites that fill your sales funnel before groundbreaking.")

## Stage of business

**Stage: No portfolio**

The correct offer shape is: "I'll design your homepage upfront, for free. If you love it, you pay. If not, you walk. No invoice, no awkward follow-up."

## Offer mechanics

**Risk reversal:** Free homepage design upfront. Paid on approval only.

**Price anchor (3-tier, they buy the middle):**

| Tier | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Homepage only, no SEO, 2 revision rounds | $800 |
| Growth (recommend this) | Full 5-page site, basic local SEO, interactive lot/unit availability map, floor plan download gate, mobile-optimized | $1,800 |
| Pro | Full site + GBP optimization + 3-month SEO content plan + automation setup (buyer inquiry form + auto-reply with floor plan PDF) | $3,500 |

**Payment terms (Stage 1):** Free design upfront. On approval: 50% deposit, 50% on launch.

**The Starbucks guarantee:** "If I waste your time, I'll send you a $25 Starbucks gift card."

---

## Customer language to echo in every DM

1. "Is this the right neighborhood for my family?"
2. "How do I know the builder is reputable?"
3. "What does the finished community actually look like?"
4. "Can I see the floor plans before I commit?"
5. "Are there any lots left in my price range?"
6. "When will construction be done?"
7. "What's included in the base price?"
8. "I want to see photos of completed homes, not renderings."

---

## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Completed project gallery** (buyers need to see finished homes and real communities, not just architect renderings, before they trust a developer)
2. **Interactive lot availability map** (buyers want to self-serve and find out what is left without calling a sales rep, so they can move at their own pace)
3. **Builder credentials and timeline transparency** (years in business, number of completed communities, and a plain-language build timeline remove the biggest fear: that the project stalls or the developer disappears)
