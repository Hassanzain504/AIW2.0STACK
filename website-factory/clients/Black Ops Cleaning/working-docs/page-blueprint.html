How to write a local hook that is not filler

Roto-Rooter's Rockville page explains that the town has clay heavy soil so pipes shift, mature street trees so roots invade, and older neighbourhoods still on cast iron. None of that survives a find and replace to another city.

The cleaning equivalents: building stock (Town Center is newer open plan glass, the Pike corridor is 1980s office, Twinbrook is older low rise), industry mix (Shady Grove and the I-270 corridor are biotech and medical, so infection control and after hours access, not general janitorial), weather (Mid-Atlantic storms ending in sewage backup), and regulation (what a failed county infection control inspection costs a practice).

06

What every page carries

Regardless of type. These are the universal floors, and the QA agents check them on every page.

  • Utility bar and footer, with name, address and phone identical to the Google listing, character for character
  • Two CTAs reachable above the fold, one primary and one secondary
  • A first-ask form of four fields or fewer
  • A persistent CTA path on mobile, sticky call and quote bar
  • Structured data, so Google can read the business, the service and the FAQ
  • One H1 only, naming the thing and the place. Three of four live pages currently have no H1 at all
  • A unique title and meta description. The live homepage and services page currently compete with each other
  • Alt text on every image. 22 of 32 homepage images currently have none
  • Images under 200KB, WebP, page under 1MB. The live homepage is 7.6MB
  • Call tracking on every phone number

Build order

Service pages first, because they are the deepest and everything links to them. Then town pages, which need the service pages to exist before their service blocks can link anywhere. Then service plus town, which need both parents live so they can link up and stay thin. Supporting pages fit around them, except Government Contracting, which should go early because it is the one that is also a traffic page.

Home is rebuilt inside engagement one as part of the technical work, then finished as a full page once the services it routes to actually exist.
