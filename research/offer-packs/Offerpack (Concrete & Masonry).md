## Top 3 trust elements to name in every DM

1. **Project gallery with finish type, application, and investment range** (concrete and masonry buyers are comparing stamped vs broom finish, pavers vs poured -- a labeled gallery converts the undecided homeowner faster than any headline)
2. **Warranty statement in plain language** (concrete that cracks is the #1 negative review trigger; a written warranty on workmanship and materials removes the fear that drives homeowners to pick the cheapest quote)
3. **Named contractor with years of experience and license** (concrete is permanent; homeowners pouring a foundation or a driveway want to know the person behind the quote has a track record, not just a truck)

## The one outreach rule

Send the short DM first on Instagram or LinkedIn. If no reply in 4 days, send the medium version to their email. If no reply in another 5 days, send the long version with the Starbucks offer. Three touches per prospect, then move on.

Do not pitch. Ask for 15 minutes. The goal of the DM is not to sell a website. The goal is to get a screen-share call where you show them the free design.
