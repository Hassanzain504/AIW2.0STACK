## How to check from a specific location

Searching for "rockville" from Pakistan is not the same as searching from Rockville. Google
weights the searcher's physical location heavily, especially for the map pack. To see what a
local actually sees:

**Chrome DevTools method, free:**
1. Open an incognito window
2. F12 to open DevTools
3. Ctrl+Shift+P, type "sensors", open "Show Sensors"
4. Under Location, choose "Other" and enter the latitude and longitude from the tables above
5. Reload, then search on google.com

**Faster alternative:** append `&uule=` location encoding, or just use a rank tracker with a
city-level location setting. Semrush Position Tracking set to Rockville, Maryland at city level
does this automatically, which is why that project is worth setting up.

**For the real picture:** a single ranking position hides the truth in local. A business can be
number one at its own pin and invisible two miles away. Local Falcon runs a grid of scan points
across the town and shows the ranking at each one. It is cheap per scan and it is the report
that sells a retainer, because the before and after grids are impossible to argue with.

## What the geography means for the plan

1. **Rockville and the 8 mile ring is the map pack fight.** Winnable, months 1 to 6, gated by
   reviews.
2. **DC, Arlington and Alexandria are not map pack targets.** Any promise made about them is a
   promise about organic city pages, on a 12 month timeline.
3. **The specialty lane ignores most of this.** Biohazard and trauma searches often show no map
   pack, buyers accept longer travel, and organic pages can cover the whole DMV. This is the
   one lane where geography is not the constraint.
4. **City page build order follows Ring 1 first**, not the biggest city first. Rockville, North
   Bethesda, Gaithersburg, Bethesda, then outward. Building a Washington DC page in month one
   is effort spent where it cannot pay off yet.
