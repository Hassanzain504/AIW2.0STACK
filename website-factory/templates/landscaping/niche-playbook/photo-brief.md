# Photo Brief - Landscaping Niche
Required photography before the build agent starts Stage 10.1.

---

## Minimum photo requirements

This is a visual niche. A landscaping site without real photos cannot perform. Do not start the build without at least the minimum set below.

### Required (build cannot proceed without these)

1. **Hero image** - 1 photo minimum, 3 ideal
   - Finished exterior project (patio, full yard, garden transformation)
   - Daylight, well-lit, no blur, no distortion
   - Landscape orientation, at least 1500x900px
   - No people required but natural-looking scene preferred

2. **Gallery photos** - 10 photos minimum, 20 ideal
   - Mix of project types: at least 2 per service category offered
   - Consistent daylight conditions
   - No blurry phone shots
   - Format: JPG, at least 1200x900px

3. **Before/after pairs** - 3 pairs ideal, 1 minimum
   - Both photos from the exact same angle and distance
   - Label each pair clearly when handing to the build agent

### Strongly recommended

4. **Owner headshot** - 1 photo
   - Outdoor setting preferred (yard, job site)
   - Natural light
   - Professional but not corporate (real gear is fine)

5. **Crew in action** - 1-2 photos
   - Team working on a real job
   - Branded shirts or hats preferred
   - Safety and professionalism visible

6. **Trust/credibility assets**
   - Association logos (NALP, PLANET, BBB, local chamber) if applicable
   - Award or certificate scans if applicable
   - License certificate (not shown publicly but used for trust copy)

---

## What to do if the client has no photos

Do not proceed with the build. Tell the student to pause and request a 1-hour site-documentation shoot before continuing. A phone camera in good daylight is sufficient. The shoot does not require a professional photographer.

Suggested shot list for a 1-hour phone shoot:
1. Best finished project from 3 angles (wide, mid, close detail)
2. Before photo of a current or upcoming project
3. Owner headshot in working environment
4. Crew working (2-3 candid shots)
5. Any signage, truck, or branded equipment

---

## File delivery format

- JPG, quality 90+
- Named descriptively: `patio-natural-stone-before.jpg`, `lawn-care-weekly-service.jpg`
- Delivered to: `clients/[Client Name]/[Client Name] Assets/photos/`
- Subdirectories: `hero/`, `gallery/`, `team/`, `before-after/`, `logos/`
