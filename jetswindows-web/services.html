Frequently asked questions

How much does window cleaning cost in Richmond?
Every home is different, so we give free, no-obligation quotes based on the number of windows, stories, and access. Most homeowners find our pricing fair and transparent — the quote you approve is the price you pay.
Do you clean windows inside and out?
Yes. Our standard residential service covers interior and exterior glass, with screens and sills included for a complete, streak-free finish.
Is pressure washing safe for my driveway and siding?
For hard surfaces like concrete driveways and patios we use pressure washing. For siding, we use gentle soft washing so we never force water behind panels or damage paint.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes, Jet's is fully licensed and insured, so your home and our crew are protected on every job.
What if I'm not satisfied?
We back every job with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If something isn't right, we come back and make it right.
