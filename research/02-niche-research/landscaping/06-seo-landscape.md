- City + service: "landscape design Phoenix," "lawn care Austin," "patio installation Chicago"
- Project-type + city: "synthetic grass installation Phoenix," "paver patio contractor Austin"
- Design/cost research: "backyard landscape design cost [city]," "how much does landscaping cost [state]"

## Schema Markup Recommendations

1. **LocalBusiness schema** — NAP, hours, service area, rating. Required.
2. **Service schema** — One per service page (Landscape Design, Lawn Care, Patio Installation, Synthetic Grass, Hardscape).
3. **FAQPage schema** — For cost and vetting questions from PAA data.
4. **Review/AggregateRating schema** — Star display in organic results.
5. **ImageObject schema** — On gallery pages with before/after photos; helps images appear in Google Image search (meaningful for a visual niche).
6. **BreadcrumbList schema** — For multi-service or city/suburb sub-page structure.

## Content Gaps

1. **Honest local cost guides.** "What is a realistic landscaping budget?" and "Why is landscape design so expensive?" are unanswered by contractor sites in the SERP. A contractor who publishes "What Landscaping Costs in [City] in 2026 — From Our Last 150 Projects" with honest budget ranges by project type gets the research visit before competitors.

2. **"How to pick a good landscaper" guide.** This PAA question appears directly. A contractor who publishes an honest vetting guide (including "ask to see a written contract before paying any deposit") builds authority with the exact homeowner who has been burned before.

3. **Desert/climate-specific content (Phoenix and similar markets).** "Desert landscaping," "water-wise landscaping," "low-maintenance landscaping in Phoenix" are underserved by contractor content. In the Phoenix market specifically, synthetic grass and drought-tolerant planting are major project types with dedicated search intent.

4. **Project-type portfolio pages.** Most contractor sites have one generic gallery. Individual pages for "Patio Installation in [City]," "Synthetic Grass in [City]," and "Backyard Fire Pit Projects" capture project-specific search intent and give homeowners a browsable reference.

---

## GBP Priority

Landscaping "near me" searches are dominated by aggregators and local pack results. GBP optimization is the primary ranking strategy for local visibility:
- All service categories populated
- High-quality before/after photos uploaded regularly
- Q&A populated with PAA-matching questions
- Review velocity: consistent new reviews matter more than volume
